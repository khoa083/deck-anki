@title_vi: Thành phố
@theory
## V1 | Language chunks | Các cụm ngôn ngữ
COLLOCATIONS = words frequently used together (*densely populated, leafy suburbs, open spaces, violent crime*); COMPOUND NOUNS = nouns made of two or more words (*tourist attraction, skyscraper, cost of living*); DEPENDENT PREPOSITIONS (*steeped in history, plagued by crime*); PHRASAL VERBS (*break down, get around, look forward to*); IDIOMS (*go to town on something, hit the road, cut and dried*). ‖ Kết hợp từ, danh từ ghép, giới từ đi kèm, cụm động từ, thành ngữ.
## V2 | Adjectives as noun suffixes | Tính từ làm hậu tố
*-free* = without an undesirable thing (*fat-free*); *-friendly* = suitable for or welcoming of (*child-friendly*); *-mad* = liking or doing something obsessively (*football-mad*). The noun in these phrases is singular. ‖ *-free* (không có), *-friendly* (thân thiện với), *-mad* (mê); danh từ ở số ít.
## V3 | people / persons | people / persons
*people* is the usual plural of *person*; *persons* is rare in speech but used in formal contexts (*by person or persons unknown*). ‖ Số nhiều thường dùng *people*; *persons* chỉ trong ngữ cảnh trang trọng.
