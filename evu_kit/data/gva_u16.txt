@title_vi: Mệnh đề danh từ
@theory
## 2.1 | Noun clauses | Mệnh đề danh từ
A noun clause functions like a noun or noun phrase; usually begins with *that* or a wh-word (*what, when, where, which, who, why, whether, whatever, how*). ‖ Mệnh đề danh từ có chức năng như danh từ; bắt đầu bằng *that* hoặc từ để hỏi.
## 2.2 | That-noun clauses | Mệnh đề danh từ với that
*that* often left out informally; *the fact that* when the clause is subject, after a preposition or after *change, face (up to), overlook*; also *the argument / assumption / belief / claim / idea / notion / view that*. ‖ Có thể bỏ *that*; dùng *the fact that* làm chủ ngữ, sau giới từ; *the idea that*.
## 2.3 | Wh-noun clauses | Mệnh đề danh từ với wh-
*of* + wh-word after nouns like *example, problem*; object before wh-word with *advise, teach*; *how* after *decide, know*; wh-clause (not that-clause) after a preposition; *whatever, whoever, whichever* for indefinite things/people; alternatives *the reason (why), something which, the place, the time, the way, somebody*. ‖ *the example of what…*; *advise you when…*; sau giới từ dùng wh-; *whatever, whoever*.
## 2.4 | Whether and if | Whether và if
*whether / if* for possible choices (vs *that* for facts); formal *as to whether*; only *whether* before *or not*, before a to-infinitive, usually after a preposition and after *advise, choose, consider, debate, discuss, enquire, question*, and as subject or complement. ‖ *whether* (không *if*) trước *or not*, to V, sau giới từ, làm chủ ngữ.
