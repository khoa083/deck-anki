@theory
## V1 | at last and finally | at last và finally
Both mean something happens after a long period of waiting or effort (*At last I'm old enough to drive. I've finally finished my essay.*). Only *finally* introduces the last point in writing or a speech (*Finally, I'd like to thank you all…*; not *At last*). ‖ Đánh dấu ý cuối chỉ dùng *finally*.
## V2 | Compound nouns replacing phrases | Danh từ ghép thay cụm dài
Compound nouns can replace a longer phrase with a preposition: *power supply* = supply of power; *gas emissions, petrol tank, family car, car manufacturers, oil resources, diesel engines*. ‖ Danh từ ghép thay cụm có giới từ.
## V3 | Branches of science | Các ngành khoa học
*anatomy* (body), *anthropology* (cultures, customs), *archaeology* (remains, settlements), *astronomy* (comets, galaxies), *ecology* (habitats, species), *genetics* (chromosomes, heredity), *meteorology* (weather), *psychology* (mind, behaviour), *seismology* (earthquakes), *zoology* (animals). ‖ Các ngành khoa học và từ vựng liên quan.
