@title_vi: Nói năng
@theory
## A | Say (said) | Say
Use *say* to report someone’s words (*She said, ‘This is horrible!’ He said that he wanted a drink.*), for set phrases (*say hello / goodbye / please / thank you*) and to ask about language (*How do you say ‘book’ in Spanish?*). *Say* is not followed immediately by a person. ‖ *say* để thuật lời, nói các câu cố định, hỏi về ngôn ngữ; không đặt người ngay sau *say*.
## B | Tell (told) | Tell
*Tell* is usually followed by a person: *He told me his name. Tell me when… She told me how to…* You *tell someone the time / a story / a joke*. *Can you tell me where…?* (not *Can you say me*). ‖ *tell* + người: *kể, bảo, nói cho ai*.
## C | Ask | Ask
*Ask* is used for questions: *ask someone a question, ask someone the way / the time, ask somebody to do something, ask for something*. ‖ *ask* để hỏi: *hỏi ai, hỏi đường, nhờ ai làm gì, xin cái gì*.
## D | Speak / talk / answer / reply | Speak / talk / answer / reply
*talk to* someone (have a conversation); *speak* a language (not *talk Japanese*); *answer* the phone / the door / a question; *reply to* an email. ‖ *nói chuyện với; nói (ngôn ngữ); nghe máy, ra mở cửa, trả lời; hồi âm*.
