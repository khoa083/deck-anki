@title_vi: Mệnh đề trạng ngữ
@theory
## 2.1 | Adverbial clauses: general | Mệnh đề trạng ngữ: tổng quát
An adverbial clause adds information (time, reason, purpose…) to a main clause, usually begins with a conjunction, can come before or after it; it can’t be a separate sentence; only one conjunction. ‖ Mệnh đề trạng ngữ bổ sung thời gian, lý do, mục đích; không đứng riêng; chỉ một liên từ.
## 2.2 | Time clauses | Mệnh đề thời gian
No *will* after time conjunctions (*before, until, when*); *when* (not *as / while*) for a point in time, circumstances, a past period of life, ‘every time’ (*whenever*); *as* for two things changing together; *as / when* for the moment something happens; *as* can mean ‘because’ or ‘while’. ‖ Không dùng *will*; *when* cho thời điểm, hoàn cảnh, giai đoạn đời; *as* cho thay đổi song song.
## 2.3 | Contrast and concession clauses | Mệnh đề tương phản, nhượng bộ
*although / though* (*though* at the end of a clause), *despite / in spite of the fact that*, *despite / in spite of (my) + -ing*, *while / whereas* (formal *whilst*). ‖ *although, though, despite the fact that, whereas*.
## 2.4 | Reason clauses | Mệnh đề lý do
*because, so, because of* + noun; formal *as, since*; informal *seeing that / seeing as*. ‖ *because, as, since, seeing that*.
## 2.5 | Purpose and result clauses | Mệnh đề mục đích
*in order / so as + to-infinitive* (informally just to-infinitive); negatives *in order not to / so as not to*; *in order that / so (that)* + often modal verbs. ‖ *in order to, so as not to, so that + can/would*.
