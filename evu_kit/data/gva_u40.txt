@title_vi: Thế giới việc làm
@theory
## V1 | Leaving a job | Rời công việc
*make an employee redundant / lay off* (informal) = the employer no longer needs staff; *dismiss / fire / sack* (informal) = the employee is unsuitable; *resign* = the employee chooses to leave; *retire* = the employee has come to the end of their career. ‖ *redundant/lay off* (cắt giảm), *dismiss/fire/sack* (sa thải), *resign* (từ chức), *retire* (nghỉ hưu).
## V2 | job vs work | job và work
*job* is countable = a particular employment (*got a job as a magazine editor*); *work* is uncountable = the general activity in which we use effort (*so much work to do*); *work* is also a verb. ‖ *job* đếm được; *work* không đếm được.
