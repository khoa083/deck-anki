@title_vi: Động từ khuyết thiếu (1)
@theory
## 2.1 | Can, could, be able to: ability | Khả năng
*be able to* instead of *can/could* (more formal); *be able to* for ability on a specific past occasion (*were able to*, not *could*); *couldn’t / weren’t able to* in negatives; *can/could* with verbs of sense and thinking; *be able to* in perfect tenses, -ing forms, infinitives, after modals; *can/could* in passives; *will be able to* for future ability; *can / be able to* for future arrangements; *can/could* for permission. ‖ *be able to* trang trọng hơn; dịp cụ thể trong quá khứ dùng *was/were able to*; *can/could* với động từ giác quan; *will be able to* cho tương lai.
## 2.2 | May, might, can, could: possibility | Khả năng xảy ra
*can/may* for general possibility; *could* for past possibility; not *may* in questions (use *could* or *be likely to*); negatives with *only, hardly*: *can hardly be described*, *may/might not*. ‖ *can/may*: khả năng chung; *could*: khả năng quá khứ; hỏi dùng *could / be likely to*.
## 2.3 | Will, would, used to: conclusions, willingness, habitual events | Kết luận, sẵn lòng, thói quen
*will* for conclusions/assumptions; *will (not)* for (un)willingness/refusal (also machines); *would* for future willingness, conditionals, willing but unable (not for a specific past occasion); *will/would* for characteristic behaviour; *would / used to* for repeated past actions, only *used to* for past states. ‖ *will* suy đoán; *won’t* từ chối; *would / used to* thói quen quá khứ; trạng thái quá khứ chỉ *used to*.
## 2.4 | Must, have (got) to: necessity, deduction | Cần thiết, suy luận
*must / have to* (have to less formal, preferred in questions); *had to* for past; *will have to* for future; *must* = speaker’s opinion, *have to* = external rule; *must* for conclusions, *can’t / couldn’t* for impossibility; *have got to* informal (contracted *’ve got to*); *had to* not *had got to*. ‖ *must* (ý người nói), *have to* (quy định bên ngoài); suy luận *must have*, phủ định *can’t*.
## 2.5 | Didn’t need to, didn’t have to, needn’t have | Không cần thiết
*didn’t need to / didn’t have to* = not necessary (and not done); *needn’t have* = done but not necessary. ‖ *didn’t need to*: không cần (và không làm); *needn’t have*: đã làm nhưng không cần.
## 2.6 | Should, ought to: obligation | Bổn phận
*should / ought to* for advice, recommendations, responsibility; for things likely because planned or expected; *shouldn’t* (not *oughtn’t to*) for unlikely things. ‖ *should / ought to*: khuyên, trách nhiệm, điều có khả năng; *shouldn’t*: điều khó xảy ra.
