@title_vi: Tâm trạng
@theory
## V1 | Gradable and extreme adjectives | Tính từ có mức độ và tính từ cực điểm
Gradable adjectives (*angry*) can be made stronger or weaker with *very, quite, extraordinarily, a bit, rather, mildly*. Extreme adjectives (*furious*) include the idea 'very', so we use adverbs meaning 100%: *absolutely, completely, thoroughly, utterly, totally* (not *very furious*). *Really* works with both. Note collocations: *faintly ludicrous, thoroughly miserable, bitterly disappointing, endlessly fascinating*. ‖ Tính từ cực điểm không dùng *very*, dùng *absolutely, totally*…
## V2 | remember + to-infinitive / -ing | remember + to V / V-ing
*remember to do* = it is a fact, you did it (*He remembered to phone work*); *remember doing* = it is a memory (*He remembers phoning work*). ‖ *remember to V* (nhớ để làm); *remember V-ing* (nhớ đã làm).
