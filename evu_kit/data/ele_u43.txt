@title_vi: Take / took / taken
@theory
## A | Take with time | Take nói về thời gian
*It + take + person + time*: *It takes Alan 20 minutes to get to work. It took me two hours.* *How long does it take to get to the station? How long did it take you to learn…?* ‖ *Mất bao lâu*: *It takes sb + thời gian + to do sth*.
## B | Take something with you | Mang theo
*Take an umbrella. Take some water with you. You can’t take your camera into the museum.* ‖ *mang theo* ô, nước; không được *mang* máy ảnh vào bảo tàng.
## C | Expressions | Cách nói
*take a photo, take a course, take an exam, take lessons, take the bus / the underground / a taxi / the train*. Tip: keep a notebook page for *take* (*take a look at, take a chance*). ‖ *chụp ảnh, theo học khóa, dự thi, học, đi xe buýt / tàu điện ngầm / taxi / tàu*.
