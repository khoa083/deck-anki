@title_vi: Have / had / had
@theory
## A | What can you have? | Có thể “have” những gì?
*have* + meals (*breakfast, lunch, dinner, a meal*), events (*a party, a meeting, a game*), study (*a lesson, an exam, homework*), food and drink (*a cup of tea, a drink, an ice cream*), washing (*a shower, a bath, a swim*). ‖ *have* + bữa ăn, sự kiện, học tập, đồ ăn uống, tắm rửa.
## B | Expressions with have | Cách nói với have
*have a look, have a go, have a good journey, have a moment, have a word with, have a good time, have my hair cut, don’t have the time*. ‖ *xem thử, thử làm, chúc thượng lộ bình an, có chút thời gian, nói chuyện với, vui vẻ, đi cắt tóc, không có thời gian*.
## C | have got = have | have got = have
*Have got* is spoken / informal: *I’ve got three sisters. Have you got a pen? We haven’t got any.* ‖ *Have got* dùng khi nói / thân mật.
## D | have got to = have to | have got to = have to
Use *have (got) to* when you are obliged to do something: *You have to / you’ve got to pay $10.* In the past use *had to* (not *had got to*). Tip: group *have* expressions by topic. ‖ *have (got) to* = phải; quá khứ dùng *had to*. Mẹo: nhóm cụm *have* theo chủ đề.
