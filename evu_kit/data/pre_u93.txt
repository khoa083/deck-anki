@title_vi: Bổ sung và tương phản
@theory
## A | As well (as), what’s more, in addition (to), besides | Thêm thông tin
*also, as well (syn too), as well as (+ -ing), what’s more, in addition, in addition to, besides (+ -ing)*. ‖ *cũng, ngoài ra, hơn nữa, thêm vào đó*.
## B | Although, despite, in spite of | Mặc dù
*although / even though* + clause; *despite the fact that* + clause; *despite / in spite of* + noun / -ing; *still* emphasises surprise. ‖ *mặc dù, bất chấp; vẫn*.
## C | However, yet, though | Tuy nhiên
*however* (two sentences, different positions), *yet*, *though* (spoken, at the end). ‖ *tuy nhiên, vậy mà, dù vậy*.
## D | While and whereas | Trong khi
compare two facts: *Alex is quick, whereas/while the others are slow*. ‖ *trong khi đó*.
