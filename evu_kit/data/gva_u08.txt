@title_vi: So sánh
@theory
## 2.1 | Adjectives and adverbs: comparative and superlative forms | Dạng so sánh hơn và nhất
*-er/-est* for one-syllable adjectives (but *more/most* with participle adjectives and *fun, real, right, wrong*); *-er/-est* or *more/most* for two-syllable adjectives (always *more/most* with participle adjectives, *-ful, -less*, and *afraid, alone, cautious, famous, modern…*); *more/most* for three+ syllables (but *unhappier, unlikelier…* possible); *the + comparative…, the + comparative…*; *less / least* as opposite; informally *not as … as*; adverbs mostly *more/most* (but *harder, faster*). ‖ *-er/-est* với tính từ một âm tiết; *more/most* với tính từ phân từ, *-ful/-less*, ba âm tiết; *the… the…*; *less/least*; trạng từ thường dùng *more/most*.
## 2.2 | Superlatives: special cases | So sánh nhất: trường hợp đặc biệt
*of* + plural words, *in/on* + singular places/groups; *of*-phrase at the front for emphasis; *the* can be left out after a linking verb informally (*Which one’s cheapest?*). ‖ *of* + số nhiều, *in/on* + nơi chốn; có thể bỏ *the* sau động từ nối.
## 2.3 | Comparisons with as … | So sánh với as … as
*as … as* (equally); degree words *about, almost, just, nearly, nowhere near, not nearly*; *not as / not so*; *as much/many as, as little/few as* (more/less than expected); *as … as possible / ever / usual*; *as + adjective + a/an + noun + as*; *not as big a problem* / *not such a bad idea*; *as + clause*; *as* (role, function) vs *like* (similar to). ‖ *as … as*; *nowhere near as*; *as many as 12*; *as cheap a flight as possible*; *as* (vai trò) khác *like* (giống).
## 2.4 | Comparisons with so …, too …, enough | So sánh với so, too, enough
*so + adjective + that-clause* (formal *so … as to + infinitive*), *too + adjective + to-infinitive*, *adjective + enough + to-infinitive*. ‖ *so… that*, *too… to*, *… enough to*.
