## V1 | species, series | species, series
*species* has the same form in the singular and plural (*an endangered species; thousands of species of bees*); *series* is similar (*the new wildlife series; four series of documentaries*). ‖ *species, series*: số ít và số nhiều giống nhau.
## V2 | provide sb with sth | provide sb with sth
*Plants provide (us) with the air we breathe.* (not *provide us the air*) ‖ *provide sb with sth*, không *provide sb sth*.
## V3 | Animal groups | Các nhóm động vật
Fish use gills to breathe and live only in water; mammals drink their mother's milk; amphibians live partly on land and partly underwater; reptiles have dry, scaly skin and are cold-blooded; insects have six legs and their skeleton on the outside; birds have feathers and lay eggs. ‖ Cá, thú, lưỡng cư, bò sát, côn trùng, chim.
