@title_vi: Cuộc họp 5: kỹ thuật thảo luận
@theory
## A | Interrupting, referring back, checking understanding, avoiding confrontation | Ngắt lời, nhắc lại, kiểm tra hiểu, tránh đối đầu
Check: *Are you saying / suggesting that…?, If I understand (you) correctly…, If I follow you…*; avoid *confrontation*: *I take your point…, I understand what you’re saying…, I see/know what you mean, but…, I hear where you’re coming from on this, but…*; interrupt: *If I can just stop you for a moment, Can I (just) come in here?, Sorry to interrupt you, but…*; refer back: *To go back to what you were saying earlier…, As we were saying earlier…* ‖ *Ý bạn là…?; Nếu tôi hiểu đúng…; Tôi hiểu ý bạn, nhưng…; Cho tôi ngắt lời chút; Xin lỗi ngắt lời; Quay lại điều bạn nói lúc nãy…*
## B | Agreement, consensus or compromise? | Thỏa thuận, đồng thuận hay thỏa hiệp?
*agreement (agree about / on), compromise (n/v), consensus, have a disagreement about, disagree about*; *come to / reach / find (an) agreement / compromise / consensus*. ‖ *thỏa thuận, thỏa hiệp, đồng thuận, bất đồng; đạt được thỏa thuận*.
## C | Concluding | Kết thúc
*We’ve covered a lot of ground; It’s been a very interesting discussion; We’re running out of time; To go over what’s been said…; To sum up…; We’re going to have to agree to disagree; Unless anyone has anything else to add…; We’re going to have to stop there; I think that’s it; Thank you all for coming*. ‖ *Ta đã bàn nhiều; Sắp hết giờ; Điểm lại…; Tóm lại…; Đành chấp nhận bất đồng; Nếu không ai thêm gì…; Đành dừng ở đây*.
