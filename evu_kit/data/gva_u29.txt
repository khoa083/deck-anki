@title_vi: Di cư
@theory
## V1 | Prefixes en- and em- | Tiền tố en- và em-
*en-* and *em-* add the meaning 'put into', 'cause to be' or 'provide with' to verbs: *endanger* = put in danger; also *encase, encircle, engulf, enable, enlarge, enliven, enrich, enclose*. *em-* is used before *b* or *p*: *embed, embitter, embody, empower*. ‖ *en-/em-* = đặt vào, làm cho, cung cấp; *em-* trước b, p.
## V2 | many vs a lot of / lots of | many và a lot of / lots of
*Many* is common in affirmative sentences in writing, but very formal in spoken affirmative sentences, where *a lot of / lots of* are preferred; *many* is used in questions and negatives at any level of formality. ‖ Nói khẳng định dùng *a lot of*; viết hoặc câu hỏi/phủ định dùng *many*.
## V3 | Prefix multi- | Tiền tố multi-
*multi-* means 'many': *multicultural, multilingual, multinational, multicoloured, multifunction(al)*. ‖ *multi-* = nhiều.
