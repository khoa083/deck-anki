@title_vi: Từ hạn định và từ chỉ lượng
@theory
## 2.1 | No, none, not a, not any | No, none, not a, not any
*no* (= not any), *none of* (= not any of); *neither of* for two; at the start of a clause use *no / none of*, not *not a / not any*; *none* without a noun when clear. ‖ *no*, *none of*; hai người/vật dùng *neither of*; đầu mệnh đề không dùng *not any*.
## 2.2 | Much, many, a lot of, lots of | Much, many, a lot of
*much (of)* + uncountable, *many (of)* + plural, mainly in negatives and questions (formal positives too); *a lot (of) / lots (of)* in positives; *much / many* without a noun; *much of / many of* = a large part / number of; *many* with time expressions and ‘number + of’. ‖ *much/many* ở câu phủ định, nghi vấn; *a lot of* câu khẳng định; *many hours, many hundreds of*.
## 2.3 | All, both, whole | All, both, whole
*all* after *be* / first auxiliary, before main verb, after the noun; *both (of)* positive / *neither (of)* negative for two; *the whole* before singular countable nouns; *all* before *day, week, night*; *all the / the whole* + *way, time*. ‖ Vị trí *all*; *both/neither* cho hai; *the whole* + danh từ số ít; *all day*.
## 2.4 | Every, each | Every, each
*each (of)* for two or more, *every* for three or more; *every* with *almost, nearly, practically, virtually* and regular intervals (*every other, every few weeks*); *each* for both in a pair, as a pronoun. ‖ *every* (từ ba trở lên, khoảng đều đặn); *each* (cặp, đại từ).
## 2.5 | (A / the) few, little; less, fewer (than) | Few, little, less, fewer
*few / little* formal (= not many / not much); *a few / a little* = enough or more than expected; *few / little* = not enough; *less* + uncountable, *fewer* + plural; *fewer than* (not *less than*) with plural nouns in exams; *less than* for time, distance, money. ‖ *a few* (đủ), *few* (không đủ); *fewer* + số nhiều; *less than* cho thời gian, khoảng cách, tiền.
## 2.6 | Much, many, both, all, each, none, few, little + (of) | … + of
*of* needed before a pronoun, a determiner, a possessive; informally *of* can be left out after *both / all* before *the, these, my…* but not before *them, you, us*. ‖ Cần *of* trước đại từ, từ hạn định, sở hữu.
