@title_vi: Truyền thông
@theory
## V1 | channel | channel
Original meaning: a passage for water (*The English Channel*); now also a way to communicate information (*diplomatic channel, television channel*); as a verb: do something in a particular direction (*channel his energy into something more positive*). ‖ Eo biển; kênh thông tin; (động từ) dồn vào.
## V2 | big vs large, great, wide | big và large, great, wide
LARGE: *a large number / quantity / amount*; GREAT: *great pleasure / importance / fun / success*; WIDE: *a wide choice / range / variety*; BIG: *a big increase / improvement / problem / surprise / impact*. ‖ Không lạm dụng *big*.
## V3 | Adverbs strengthening opinions | Trạng từ nhấn mạnh ý kiến
*I thoroughly approve of the idea. I wholeheartedly support…; …will undoubtedly help…; I honestly don't think…; I genuinely believe…; I fundamentally disagree with…* ‖ Trạng từ làm mạnh ý kiến.
