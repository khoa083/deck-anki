@title_vi: Cảm xúc
@theory
## A | Love, like and hate | Yêu, thích và ghét
From strongest to weakest: *love* (++), *like* (+), *don’t like* (–), *hate* (– –). *I love my family. I hate spiders.* ‖ Từ mạnh đến yếu: *yêu*, *thích*, *không thích*, *ghét*.
## B | Happy, sad and tired | Vui, buồn và mệt
- *happy, sad, angry, upset, cold, hot, thirsty, hungry, well, ill, tired, surprised* ‖ vui, buồn, giận, buồn bực, lạnh, nóng, khát, đói, khỏe, ốm, mệt, ngạc nhiên
*happy about* your news; *happy for* you (you did well). ‖ *happy about* (vui về) tin của bạn; *happy for* (mừng cho) bạn.
## C | Prefer, hope and want | Thích hơn, hy vọng, muốn
*I prefer coffee to tea. I hope to do well / I hope (that) my friend does well. I want a new car / to buy a car / my mother to buy a car.* Not *I want that you help me*. ‖ *prefer A to B* (thích A hơn B); *hope to do / hope (that)…*; *want sth / to do / sb to do*. Không nói *want that…*.
## D | Expressions | Cách nói
*Do you like football? – Yes, I really like it. / No, I don’t like it very much.* (not *I very like* / *I like very much football*). *How’s your grandfather? – He’s very well. – She’s a bit tired.* ‖ *Bạn thích bóng đá không? – Có, rất thích. / Không thích lắm.* Không nói *I very like*.
