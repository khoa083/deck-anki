@title_vi: Danh từ hóa
@theory
## 2.1 | Nominalisation | Danh từ hóa
Using a noun or noun phrase for an idea usually expressed by a verb or adjective; common in formal writing. An adverb modifying a verb becomes an adjective (*rose suddenly → a sudden rise*); the main noun is often followed by prepositional phrases (*a rise in the level of…*). Used to avoid mentioning the agent, to express two clauses concisely as one, and to give a different focus (new information at the end). ‖ Dùng danh từ thay động từ/tính từ; trạng từ → tính từ; thường + cụm giới từ; tránh nêu tác nhân, cô đọng, đổi tiêu điểm.
## 2.2 | do, give, have, make, take + noun | do, give, have, make, take + danh từ
A verb can often be replaced by *do / give / have / make / take* + noun (*took immediate action = acted immediately*; *the decision was made = it was decided*); these patterns are often less formal than the verb alone (*do all the cooking*). ‖ *take action, make a decision, do the cooking* thay cho động từ; thường ít trang trọng hơn.
