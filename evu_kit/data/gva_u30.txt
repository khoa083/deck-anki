@title_vi: Mạo hiểm
@theory
## V1 | -ing compound nouns for sports | Danh từ ghép -ing chỉ môn thể thao
Compound nouns for these sporting activities are formed with the -ing form; some are two separate words (*bungee jumping*), some have a hyphen (*hang-gliding*) and others are one word (*windsurfing*). ‖ Tên môn thể thao dùng dạng -ing: tách rời, có gạch nối hoặc viết liền.
## V2 | take part in | take part in
*take part* is followed by *in*: *He's hoping to take part in the next Olympic Games.* (not *take part of*) ‖ *take part in*, không *take part of*.
## V3 | Formal language | Ngôn ngữ trang trọng
*conclusion* = decision; *determine* = find out; *display* = show; *engage in* = take part in; *foresee* = predict; *onset* = beginning; *response* = reaction, reply. ‖ Từ trang trọng và từ thường tương ứng.
## V4 | trip, slip, stumble | trip, slip, stumble
*trip* = lose your balance after hitting your foot on something; *slip* = slide unintentionally on something wet or smooth; *stumble* = put a foot down awkwardly and begin to fall. ‖ *trip* (vấp), *slip* (trượt), *stumble* (loạng choạng).
