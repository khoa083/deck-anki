@title_vi: Câu tường thuật
@theory
## 2.1 | Structures in the reported clause: that-clause | Cấu trúc mệnh đề tường thuật: that
verb + that (*reckon*); verb + object + that (*convince, tell*); verb + (object) + that (*warn*); *tell* + object + that vs *say* (+ to + object) + that; verb + that or verb + object + to-infinitive (*find*); verb + (to/with + object) + that (*admit, agree*). *that* often left out with common verbs (*reckon, say, tell, think*), but not when the clause doesn't immediately follow the verb. ‖ Các mẫu với *that*; *tell sb that* / *say (to sb) that*; có thể bỏ *that* khi mệnh đề đứng ngay sau động từ.
## 2.2 | Structures in the reported clause: to-infinitive and -ing | Cấu trúc với to V và V-ing
verb + to-inf (*decide*); verb + object + to-inf (*encourage*); verb + (object) + to-inf (*expect*); verb + to-inf or that (*promise*); verb + object + to-inf or object + that (*advise*); verb + -ing or that (*suggest*, not *suggest to*). ‖ *decide to*, *encourage sb to*, *promise to / that*, *advise sb to*, *suggest + V-ing / that*.
## 2.3 | Verb tenses in reporting | Thì trong câu tường thuật
Tenses often move back; past perfect doesn't change; present tense possible for a situation that still exists; present simple reporting verb for current news/views or what many people say. ‖ Lùi thì; quá khứ hoàn thành giữ nguyên; tình huống còn đúng có thể giữ hiện tại.
## 2.4 | Modal verbs in reporting | Động từ khuyết thiếu khi tường thuật
*will → would, can → could, may → might*; no change with a present reporting verb for current/future situations; *shall → would* (future) / *should* (suggestions, advice); *must* → *must / had to*; *could, should, would, might, ought to, used to* don't change. ‖ will→would, can→could, may→might, shall→would/should, must→had to.
## 2.5 | Reporting questions | Tường thuật câu hỏi
*asked what … were* or *asked (for) our views*; *wanted to know if/whether*; statement word order, no question mark or *do*; negative *do* for negative questions; *who Sue Ray was / who was Sue Ray*; *what to do* for *should* questions, but not for *why*. ‖ Trật tự câu trần thuật; *what to do* thay *what should we do*; không *why to*.
