@title_vi: It và there
@theory
## 2.1 | Introductory it as subject | It giới thiệu làm chủ ngữ
For weather, time, distance, situations (*It's raining*, *It's about 15 kilometres…*); as an alternative to a that-, wh-, -ing or to-infinitive clause as subject (*It's a pity that…*, *It's obvious why…*, *It's an advantage being…*, *It shocked me to see…*). Not usually instead of a noun subject in writing. Patterns: *it + verb + to-inf*, *it + verb + object + to-inf*, *it + verb + that-clause*, *it + verb + object + that*; formal *it + passive + that/wh-clause* (*It is said that…*). ‖ *It* làm chủ ngữ giả thay mệnh đề that/wh/-ing/to V; *It is said that…* (trang trọng).
## 2.2 | Introductory it as object | It giới thiệu làm tân ngữ
*verb + it + that/if/wh-clause* (*I wouldn't like it if…*, *I couldn't believe it when…*); *would appreciate it if*; *accept (it) that*; *verb + it + adjective + clause* (*make it clear whether*, *think it unlikely that*); *leave/owe it to sb to*; *see it as essential that*. ‖ *I'd appreciate it if…*; *make it clear*; *leave it to sb to…*; *see it as essential that…*.
## 2.3 | There | There
*There + be* introduces new information (existence, place) with indefinite nouns (*a/an, no article, any, some, no*); *there*, not *it*, for existence and quantities. Followed by noun + clause (*a chance that*, *plans to*), *bound/certain/likely/supposed/sure to be*; *there + modal + be*, *there seem/appear to be*, *there is expected to be*, *expect there to be*. Verb agrees with the (first) noun; informal *there's* + plural. ‖ *There + be* giới thiệu thông tin mới; động từ hợp với danh từ đầu tiên; *There's bound to be…*, *There seem to be…*.
## 2.4 | Common expressions with it's no and there's no | Thành ngữ với it's no / there's no
*it's no secret / surprise / wonder / coincidence that, it's no use/good + -ing, it's no longer necessary to, it's no exaggeration to say that…*; *there's no doubt that, there's no choice but to, there's no need to, there's no point in + -ing, there's no hurry, there's no harm in + -ing…* ‖ Các cụm cố định với *it's no* và *there's no*.
