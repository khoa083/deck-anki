@title_vi: Mẫu động từ (2)
@theory
## 2.1 | Verb + to-infinitive / -ing | Động từ + to V / V-ing
Same meaning (*begin, start, not bother, cease, continue*; avoid two -ing forms); *hate, like, love, prefer* (to-infinitive for regular actions; *would + like* + to-infinitive only); different meaning (*came to realise* vs *came swimming*); -ing after preposition *to* (*look forward to*) and phrasal-verb particles (*put off going*); position of *not* changes meaning. ‖ Nghĩa như nhau; *would like* + to V; *look forward to* + V-ing; vị trí *not* đổi nghĩa.
## 2.2 | Verb + (object) + bare infinitive | Động từ + (tân ngữ) + nguyên mẫu không to
*let / make* + object + bare infinitive (passive *be made to*); verbs of perception + object + -ing (in progress) or bare infinitive (complete); idioms *I dare say, make do with, let go of, let slip, hear tell*; *help (to)*. ‖ *make/let sb do*; *was made to do*; giác quan + V-ing/V; *help (to) do*.
## 2.3 | Verb + object + to-infinitive / -ing | Động từ + tân ngữ + to V / V-ing
Object required (*encourage, advise, persuade, tell*); no object (*decide, agree, guarantee, refuse*); *for* + object + to-infinitive (*arrange, advertise, apply, campaign, pay, wait*); -ing only with an object (*see, feel, find, hear*). ‖ Bắt buộc có tân ngữ; không có tân ngữ; *arrange for sb to do*.
## 2.4 | Verb + object / possessive + -ing | Động từ + tân ngữ / sở hữu + V-ing
*appreciate them / their looking after me* (possessive more formal, only for people, not complex objects). ‖ Sở hữu trang trọng hơn, chỉ cho người.
## 2.5 | Other patterns after verbs | Cấu trúc khác
Perfect infinitive *to have + past participle* (earlier event; things that didn’t happen: *supposed to have arrived*); perfect -ing *having + past participle* (*admit, deny, regret, remember*); continuous / passive infinitive *to be + -ing / past participle* for future actions. ‖ *to have + V3*; *having + V3*; *to be + V-ing / V3*.
