@title_vi: Trật tự từ và nhấn mạnh
@theory
## 2.1 | Fronting | Đảo lên đầu câu
Move a part of the sentence (object, adverbial) to the front to emphasise it: *This she resisted.* *For some weeks Maria had been writing to me.* ‖ Đưa tân ngữ/trạng ngữ lên đầu câu để nhấn mạnh.
## 2.2 | Cleft sentences | Câu chẻ
it-cleft: *it + is/was + emphasised part + relative clause* (*that, which, who* or none; *when, where* only informal). wh-cleft: *what-clause + is/was + emphasised part*; *all* = the only thing; usually singular *be*; can come at the end (*… was what impressed us most*); emphasise an action with *What she did was (to) + infinitive*. ‖ Câu chẻ *It is … that…*; *What … is/was…*; *All she wanted was…*; *What she did was (to)…*.
## 2.3 | Inversion | Đảo ngữ
After words with a negative meaning at the front: *never (before), rarely, seldom; barely/hardly/scarcely … when/before; no sooner … than; nowhere; neither, nor*; *only* + time expression / prepositional phrase; *at no time, on no account, under/in no circumstances, in no way*; *not only, not until, not since, not for one moment, not once, not a + noun*; *little*. Also after *first, next, now, then* with *be/come* (no inversion if a comma follows); *so + adjective … that*; *such + be … that*. Mainly formal. ‖ Đảo ngữ sau từ mang nghĩa phủ định, *only…*, *not only*, *little*, *so … that*, *such was …*.
## 2.4 | Inversion in conditional sentences | Đảo ngữ trong câu điều kiện
Formal *Were …*, *Should …*, *Had …* + subject instead of *if*; no contracted forms in negative inverted clauses (*Had Maria not…*). ‖ *Were/Should/Had* + chủ ngữ thay *if*; phủ định không viết tắt.
