## V1 | Verb suffix -ify | Hậu tố động từ -ify
*-ify* adds the meaning 'make' or 'become' to some adjectives: *purify* (= make pure), *simplify* (= make simple), *solidify* (= become solid); action nouns: *purification, simplification, solidification*; similar: *identify / identification, modify / modification, verify / verification*. ‖ *-ify* = làm cho / trở nên; danh từ *-ification*.
