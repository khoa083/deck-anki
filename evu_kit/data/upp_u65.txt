@theory
## A | Expressions for linking sentences/clauses | Cụm nối câu/mệnh đề
*In addition, Additionally, Furthermore, Moreover, What’s more, Equally, Likewise, On top of (all) that*. *Furthermore* and *moreover* are normally interchangeable, and are both rather formal; *what’s more* is informal; *what is more* is rather formal. *On top of (all) that* is emphatic: used mostly in informal spoken English. You can also use *plus* (normally used to connect noun phrases, but can connect clauses in informal speech). ‖ *Ngoài ra, thêm vào đó, hơn nữa, vả lại, tương tự, trên hết*. *Furthermore/moreover* trang trọng; *what’s more* thân mật; *what is more* khá trang trọng. *On top of (all) that* nhấn mạnh, văn nói thân mật. *Plus* thường nối cụm danh từ.
## B | Expressions at the end of sentences/clauses | Cụm cuối câu/mệnh đề
*and so on* (more informal than *etc.*), *and so on and so forth* [suggests a long continuation; informal], *into the bargain / to boot* [emphasises the combination of items; informal]. ‖ *vân vân*, *cứ thế mãi*, *lại còn… nữa*.
## C | Expressions that begin, or come in the middle of, sentences/clauses | Cụm đầu hoặc giữa câu
*Further to* (formal opening for a letter or email), *in addition to, alongside, as well as, besides / apart from, along with / together with*. ‖ *Tiếp theo* (mở đầu thư trang trọng), *ngoài… ra, song song với, cũng như, bên cạnh, cùng với*.
## LH | Language help | Lưu ý ngôn ngữ
The words and expressions in C are followed by nouns, noun phrases, or a verb plus -ing: *As well as speaking Japanese, she also speaks French* (NOT *As well as she speaks Japanese…*). ‖ Các cụm ở C theo sau là danh từ, cụm danh từ hoặc V-ing.
