**A**
**The problem**
WHAT IS THE GREENHOUSE EFFECT?
Many **greenhouse gases**, e.g. carbon dioxide, methane and ozone, **exist**1
naturally and are needed to **create**2 **the greenhouse effect** that keeps the
Earth warm enough to **support**3 human life. However, the use of **fossil**
**fuels**, e.g. **oil**, natural gas and coal, has produced **excessive**4 amounts of
greenhouse gases, and the **result**5 is **global warming**: an increase in the
average temperature on Earth. Of the 15 warmest years **on record**6, 14 have
**occurred**7 since 2000.
The **effects**8 of **climate change** can already be seen in our everyday lives.
Summers are getting hotter and winters are getting wetter, so **drought**9
and **floods**10 are becoming more common. With it, animal and plant life is
**suffering**11 – some species will disappear altogether – and certain illnesses,
e.g. hay fever, asthma and skin cancer, are becoming more common.
