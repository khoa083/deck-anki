FRIENDSHIP
Friends are people who are much more than mere **casual**
**acquaintances**1. **True friends** are always there when you need
them, as you are for them. We expect **loyalty**2 from our friends,
despite our faults, and should give it in return, never speaking
ill of them **behind their backs**3. As well as having friends and
casual acquaintances, we have relationships with **colleagues**,
**allies** and **partners.**
