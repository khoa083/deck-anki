PACIFISM Pacifists **seek** to **eradicate 1** violence and conflict, and they believe that war can never be **justified**.
A key **tenet**2 shared by **proponents**3 of this way of thinking is that life is precious and should never be wasted.
Some pacifists’ beliefs **derive from**4 their religious views, and some pacifists go as far as to oppose violence in
all forms, including in self-defence. There have been several very well-known pacifists throughout history, such as
Mahatma Gandhi, who **campaigned** for Indian independence through using non-violent **civil disobedience**5. His
success has inspired others to use peaceful **resistance** to achieve their goals, including Martin Luther King Jr., a
key figure in the Civil Rights Movement in the United States in the 1950s and 60s.
