@title_vi: I have … và I’ve got … (tôi có …)
@theory
## A | have or have got | have hoặc have got
You can say **I have** or **I’ve got**, **he has** or **he’s got**: ‖ Có thể nói **I have** hoặc **I’ve got**, **he has** hoặc **he’s got**:
|! | have | have got (short form ‖ dạng rút gọn)
| I / we / you / they | **have** | **have got** (I**’ve got** / we**’ve got** / you**’ve got** / they**’ve got**)
| he / she / it | **has** | **has got** (he**’s got** / she**’s got** / it**’s got**)
- I’ve got a headache. ‖ Tôi bị đau đầu.
- I **have** blue eyes. *or* I**’ve got** blue eyes. ‖ Tôi **có** mắt xanh.
- Tom **has** two sisters. *or* Tom **has got** two sisters. ‖ Tom **có** hai chị em gái.
- Our car **has** four doors. *or* Our car **has got** four doors. ‖ Xe của chúng tôi **có** bốn cửa.
- Sarah isn’t feeling well. She **has** a headache. *or* She**’s got** a headache. ‖ Sarah thấy không khỏe. Cô ấy **bị** đau đầu.
- They like animals. They **have** a horse, three dogs and six cats. *or* They**’ve got** a horse … ‖ Họ thích động vật. Họ **có** một con ngựa, ba con chó và sáu con mèo.
## B | I don’t have / I haven’t got etc. (negative) | I don’t have / I haven’t got v.v. (phủ định)
|! | don’t / doesn’t have | haven’t / hasn’t got
| I / you / we / they | **don’t have** | **haven’t got**
| he / she / it | **doesn’t have** | **hasn’t got**
- I **don’t have** a car. *or* I **haven’t got** a car. ‖ Tôi **không có** xe hơi.
- They **don’t have** any children. *or* They **haven’t got** any children. ‖ Họ **không có** con.
- It’s a nice house, but it **doesn’t have** a garden. *or* … it **hasn’t got** a garden. ‖ Ngôi nhà đẹp, nhưng nó **không có** vườn.
- Amy **doesn’t have** a job at the moment. *or* Amy **hasn’t got** a job … ‖ Hiện giờ Amy **không có** việc làm.
## C | do you have? / have you got? etc. (questions) | do you have? / have you got? v.v. (câu hỏi)
|! | do/does … have? | have/has … got?
| I / you / we / they | **do** I / you / we / they **have**? | **have** I / you / we / they **got**?
| he / she / it | **does** he / she / it **have**? | **has** he / she / it **got**?
- ‘**Do** you **have** a camera?’ ‘No, I **don’t**.’ *or* ‘**Have** you **got** a camera?’ ‘No, I **haven’t**.’ ‖ ‘Bạn **có** máy ảnh không?’ ‘Không.’
- ‘**Does** Helen **have** a car?’ ‘Yes, she **does**.’ *or* ‘**Has** Helen **got** a car?’ ‘Yes, she **has**.’ ‖ ‘Helen **có** xe hơi không?’ ‘Có.’
- What kind of car **does** she **have**? *or* … **has** she **got**? ‖ Cô ấy **có** loại xe gì?
- How many children **do** they **have**? *or* … **have** they **got**? ‖ Họ **có** mấy đứa con?
@rules
A: I have = I’ve got; he has = he’s got (the meaning is the same). ‖ I have = I’ve got; he has = he’s got (nghĩa như nhau).
B: Negative: I don’t have / I haven’t got; he doesn’t have / he hasn’t got. ‖ Phủ định: I don’t have / I haven’t got; he doesn’t have / he hasn’t got.
C: Questions: Do you have …? / Have you got …? Does she have …? / Has she got …? Short answers: Yes, I do / Yes, I have. ‖ Câu hỏi: Do you have …? / Have you got …? Does she have …? / Has she got …? Trả lời ngắn: Yes, I do / Yes, I have.
