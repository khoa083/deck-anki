@title_vi: Tính từ + to-infinitive, -ing, mệnh đề that, mệnh đề wh-
@theory
## A | Adjective + to-infinitive, -ing, that-clause, wh-clause | Tính từ + to-infinitive, -ing, mệnh đề that, mệnh đề wh-
When an adjective comes after a linking verb (e.g. **appear**, **be**, **become**, **seem**) we can use a number of patterns after the adjective including a **to-infinitive**, **-ing**, **that-clause**, and **wh-clause**: ‖ Khi tính từ đứng sau động từ nối (vd. **appear**, **be**, **become**, **seem**), có thể dùng nhiều cấu trúc sau tính từ, gồm **to-infinitive**, **-ing**, **mệnh đề that** và **mệnh đề wh-**:
|! adjective + ‖ tính từ + | example ‖ ví dụ | adjectives used ‖ tính từ dùng
| (i) to-infinitive ‖ = | You’re **free to leave** at any time you want. | (un)able, careful, crazy, curious, difficult, easy, foolish, free, good, hard, impossible, inclined, mad, nice, prepared, ready, stupid, welcome, willing
| (ii) -ing ‖ = | He was **busy doing** his homework. | busy, crazy, foolish, mad, stupid; (after **feel**) awful, awkward, bad, good, guilty, terrible
| (iii) that-clause ‖ mệnh đề that | He became **worried (that)** she might fall down. | afraid, alarmed, amazed, angry, annoyed, ashamed, astonished, aware, concerned, disappointed, glad, (un)happy, pleased, shocked, sorry, upset, worried; certain, confident, positive, sure
| (iv) wh-clause ‖ mệnh đề wh- | I’m not **certain (of / about) why** he wants to borrow the money. | afraid, not aware / unaware, not certain / uncertain, doubtful, not sure / unsure, worried
| (v) to-infinitive or that-clause ‖ to-infinitive hoặc mệnh đề that | She was **afraid to say** anything. / I was **afraid that** I would be late. | the adjectives in (iii), except aware, confident and positive
| (vi) to-infinitive or -ing ‖ to-infinitive hoặc -ing | He’d be **stupid to leave** now. / He’d be **stupid giving up** the job. | crazy, foolish, mad, stupid
| (vii) -ing or that-clause ‖ -ing hoặc mệnh đề that | She felt **awful leaving** him with all the clearing up. / She felt **awful that** she was late. | (after **feel**) awful, awkward, bad, good, guilty, terrible
## B | It + linking verb + adjective | It + động từ nối + tính từ
We can sometimes use **it** + linking verb + adjective + **to-infinitive** as an alternative to subject + linking verb + adjective + **to-infinitive**: ‖ Đôi khi có thể dùng **it** + động từ nối + tính từ + **to-infinitive** thay cho chủ ngữ + động từ nối + tính từ + **to-infinitive**:
- The fireworks were **amazing to watch**. *or* **It was amazing to watch** the fireworks. ‖ Pháo hoa **xem thật tuyệt**. / **Thật tuyệt khi được xem** pháo hoa.
Also: **annoying**, **awkward**, **easy**, **good**, **interesting**, **lovely**, **simple**, **terrific**, **wonderful**. ‖ Tương tự: **annoying**, **awkward**, **easy**, **good**, **interesting**, **lovely**, **simple**, **terrific**, **wonderful**.
In informal speech we can use an **-ing** form instead of a **to-infinitive**: ‖ Trong văn nói thân mật, có thể dùng **-ing** thay cho **to-infinitive**:
- **It is easy understanding** her. / **It was amazing watching** the fireworks. ‖ **Hiểu** cô ấy **thật dễ**. / **Xem** pháo hoa **thật tuyệt**.
We can use a similar pattern with adjective + **wh-** or **that-clause**: ‖ Có thể dùng cấu trúc tương tự với tính từ + mệnh đề **wh-** hoặc **that**:
- It is not **clear why** he did it. ‖ **Không rõ tại sao** anh ta làm vậy.
- It was **odd that** she left so suddenly. ‖ **Thật lạ là** cô ấy bỏ đi đột ngột như vậy.
After certain adjectives we often include **of** + subject between the adjective and a **to-infinitive**: ‖ Sau một số tính từ, ta thường thêm **of** + chủ ngữ giữa tính từ và **to-infinitive**:
- It was rude **(of them)** to criticise her. *or* They were rude to criticise her. ‖ Họ thật **thô lỗ** khi chỉ trích cô ấy.
Also: **brave**, **generous**, **kind**, **mean**, **thoughtful**, **unprofessional**, **unreasonable**. ‖ Tương tự: **brave**, **generous**, **kind**, **mean**, **thoughtful**, **unprofessional**, **unreasonable**.
When we talk about how somebody reacts to a situation we can use **it** + **make** with an adjective and **to-infinitive**, **-ing** or **that-clause**: ‖ Khi nói ai đó phản ứng với tình huống thế nào, có thể dùng **it** + **make** + tính từ + **to-infinitive**, **-ing** hoặc **mệnh đề that**:
- **It made me angry** (to discover) that so much money was wasted. (*or* It made me angry discovering that … *or* I was angry to discover that …) ‖ **Tôi tức giận** khi phát hiện ra quá nhiều tiền bị lãng phí.
Also: **ashamed**, **furious**, **glad**, **happy**, **miserable**, **nervous**, **sad**, **tired**, **uncomfortable**. ‖ Tương tự: **ashamed**, **furious**, **glad**, **happy**, **miserable**, **nervous**, **sad**, **tired**, **uncomfortable**.
@rules
A: Adjective + to-infinitive (free to leave), -ing (busy doing), that-clause (worried that), wh-clause (not certain why); afraid / sorry etc. + to-inf or that; crazy / stupid etc. + to-inf or -ing; feel awful / guilty etc. + -ing or that. ‖ Tính từ + to-infinitive (free to leave), -ing (busy doing), mệnh đề that (worried that), mệnh đề wh- (not certain why); afraid / sorry v.v. + to-inf hoặc that; crazy / stupid v.v. + to-inf hoặc -ing; feel awful / guilty v.v. + -ing hoặc that.
B: It + be + adjective + to-infinitive (informally -ing) / wh- / that-clause; It was rude / kind / brave of you to …; It made me angry / sad … to / -ing / that. ‖ It + be + tính từ + to-infinitive (thân mật: -ing) / mệnh đề wh- / that; It was rude / kind / brave of you to …; It made me angry / sad … to / -ing / that.
