@title_vi: before / after / during / while (trước / sau / trong suốt / trong khi)
@theory
## A | before, during and after | before, during và after
**before** the film / **during** the film / **after** the film ‖ **trước** bộ phim / **trong lúc** chiếu phim / **sau** bộ phim
- Everybody feels nervous **before exams**. ‖ Ai cũng hồi hộp **trước kỳ thi**.
- I fell asleep **during the film**. ‖ Tôi ngủ gật **trong lúc xem phim**.
- We were tired **after our visit** to the museum. ‖ Chúng tôi mệt **sau chuyến tham quan** bảo tàng.
## B | before, while and after | before, while và after
**before** we played / **while** we were playing / **after** we played ‖ **trước khi** chúng tôi chơi / **trong khi** chúng tôi đang chơi / **sau khi** chúng tôi chơi
- Don’t forget to close the window **before you go out**. ‖ Đừng quên đóng cửa sổ **trước khi bạn ra ngoài**.
- I often fall asleep **while I’m reading**. ‖ Tôi hay ngủ gật **trong khi đọc sách**.
- They went home **after they did the shopping**. ‖ Họ về nhà **sau khi mua sắm xong**.
## C | during, while and for | during, while và for
We use **during** + noun (during **the film**). We use **while** + verb (while **I’m reading**): ‖ Dùng **during** + danh từ (during **the film**). Dùng **while** + động từ (while **I’m reading**):
- We didn’t speak **during the meal**. ‖ Chúng tôi không nói chuyện **trong bữa ăn**.
- but We didn’t speak **while we were eating**. (*not* during we were eating) ‖ nhưng Chúng tôi không nói chuyện **trong khi ăn**. (*không* nói during we were eating)
Use **for** (*not* during) + a period of time (**three days** / **two hours** / **a year** etc.): ‖ Dùng **for** (*không* dùng during) + một khoảng thời gian (**three days** / **two hours** / **a year** v.v.):
- We played tennis **for two hours**. (*not* during two hours) ‖ Chúng tôi chơi quần vợt **hai tiếng**. (*không* nói during two hours)
- I lived in London **for a year**. (*not* during a year) ‖ Tôi sống ở London **một năm**. (*không* nói during a year)
## D | before/after + -ing | before/after + -ing
You can use **before**/**after** + **-ing** (**before going** / **after eating** etc.): ‖ Có thể dùng **before**/**after** + **-ing** (**before going** / **after eating** v.v.):
- I always have breakfast **before going** to work. (= before I go to work) ‖ Tôi luôn ăn sáng **trước khi đi** làm. (= before I go to work)
- **After doing** the shopping, they went home. (= after they did) ‖ **Sau khi mua sắm** xong, họ về nhà. (= after they did)
We say **before going** (*not* before to go), **after doing** (*not* after to do) etc.: ‖ Nói **before going** (*không* nói before to go), **after doing** (*không* nói after to do) v.v.:
- **Before eating** the apple, I washed it carefully. (*not* before to eat) ‖ **Trước khi ăn** quả táo, tôi rửa nó cẩn thận. (*không* nói before to eat)
- I started work **after reading** the newspaper. (*not* after to read) ‖ Tôi bắt đầu làm việc **sau khi đọc** báo. (*không* nói after to read)
@rules
A: before / during / after + noun (before exams, during the film, after our visit). ‖ before / during / after + danh từ (before exams, during the film, after our visit).
B: before / while / after + subject + verb (before you go out, while I’m reading). ‖ before / while / after + chủ ngữ + động từ (before you go out, while I’m reading).
C: during + noun; while + subject + verb; for + a period of time (not during). ‖ during + danh từ; while + chủ ngữ + động từ; for + khoảng thời gian (không dùng during).
D: before/after + -ing (before going, after doing) – not before to go. ‖ before/after + -ing (before going, after doing) – không dùng before to go.
