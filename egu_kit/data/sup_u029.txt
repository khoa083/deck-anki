@rules
E173: in + year / century; during + a period (the next few years, the Middle Ages, the rebuilding); for + length of time; until = up to a time; by = not later than; at that time. ‖ in + năm / thế kỷ; during + một khoảng thời gian (the next few years, the Middle Ages, the rebuilding); for + độ dài thời gian; until = cho đến khi; by = không muộn hơn; at that time.
E175: at + clock time / the same time / night / the moment; by = not later than; until = up to; in the afternoon; while + clause; for + period; on time; during the day; no preposition before last / every. ‖ at + giờ / the same time / night / the moment; by = không muộn hơn; until = cho đến khi; in the afternoon; while + mệnh đề; for + khoảng thời gian; on time; during the day; không dùng giới từ trước last / every.
E176: in a photo, at the back, in the corner, in a letter, on a page, at a disco, return to a country, on a bus, arrive at a station. ‖ in a photo, at the back, in the corner, in a letter, on a page, at a disco, return to + nước, on a bus, arrive at + nhà ga.
E177: in a flat / building / room / corner; on the ground floor / wall / door; at the end / back of. ‖ in a flat / building / room / corner; on the ground floor / wall / door; at the end / back of.
E178: on strike, the cause of, attitude to, damage to, demand for, relationship with, a rise in, need for, on a business trip, on the phone, as a shock, in connection with. ‖ on strike, the cause of, attitude to, damage to, demand for, relationship with, a rise in, need for, on a business trip, on the phone, as a shock, in connection with.
E179: as = in the role of; in the shade; on a diet; by credit card; by + agent (passive); at + speed; in capital letters; like = similar to. ‖ as = với vai trò; in the shade; on a diet; by credit card; by + tác nhân (bị động); at + tốc độ; in capital letters; like = giống như.
