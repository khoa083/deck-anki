@title_vi: go to … / go on … / go for … / go -ing
@theory
## A | go to … (go to work / go to London / go to a concert etc.) | go to … (go to work / go to London / go to a concert v.v.)
- What time do you usually **go to work**? ‖ Bạn thường **đi làm** lúc mấy giờ?
- I’m **going to China** next week. ‖ Tuần sau tôi **đi Trung Quốc**.
- Sophie didn’t want to **go to the concert**. ‖ Sophie không muốn **đi xem hòa nhạc**.
- ‘Where’s Tom?’ ‘He’s **gone to bed**.’ ‖ ‘Tom đâu?’ ‘Anh ấy **đi ngủ** rồi.’
- I **went to the dentist** yesterday. ‖ Hôm qua tôi **đi nha sĩ**.
**go to sleep** = start to sleep: ‖ **go to sleep** = bắt đầu ngủ, ngủ thiếp đi:
- I was very tired and **went to sleep** quickly. ‖ Tôi rất mệt và **ngủ thiếp đi** nhanh chóng.
**go home** (without **to**): ‖ **go home** (không có **to**):
- I’m **going home** now. (*not* going to home) ‖ Tôi **về nhà** bây giờ. (*không nói* going to home)
## B | go on … (holiday / a trip / a tour / a cruise / strike) | go on … (holiday / a trip / a tour / a cruise / strike)
- We’re **going on holiday** next week. ‖ Tuần sau chúng tôi **đi nghỉ**.
- Children often **go on school trips**. ‖ Trẻ em thường **đi dã ngoại** với trường.
- Workers at the airport have **gone on strike**. (= they are refusing to work) ‖ Công nhân sân bay đã **đình công**. (= họ từ chối làm việc)
## C | go (somewhere) for … (a walk / a run / a swim / a drink / a meal) | go (đâu đó) for … (a walk / a run / a swim / a drink / a meal)
- ‘Where’s Emma?’ ‘She’s **gone for a walk**.’ ‖ ‘Emma đâu?’ ‘Cô ấy **đi dạo** rồi.’
- Do you **go for a run** every morning? ‖ Sáng nào bạn cũng **đi chạy** à?
- The water looks nice. I’m **going for a swim**. ‖ Nước trông thích quá. Tôi **đi bơi** đây.
- I met Chris in town, so we **went for coffee**. ‖ Tôi gặp Chris trong phố, nên chúng tôi **đi uống cà phê**.
- Shall we **go** out **for a meal**? I know a good restaurant. ‖ Chúng ta **đi ăn** ngoài nhé? Tôi biết một nhà hàng ngon.
## D | go + -ing | go + -ing
We use **go** + **-ing** for many sports (**swimming** / **skiing** etc.) and also **shopping**: I go / he is going / we went / they have gone / she wants to go **shopping / swimming / fishing / sailing / skiing / jogging** etc. ‖ Dùng **go** + **-ing** cho nhiều môn thể thao (**swimming** / **skiing** v.v.) và cả **shopping**: I go / he is going / we went / they have gone / she wants to go **shopping / swimming / fishing / sailing / skiing / jogging** v.v.
- I’m going skiing. ‖ Tôi đi trượt tuyết.
- Are you **going shopping** this afternoon? ‖ Chiều nay bạn có **đi mua sắm** không?
- It’s a nice day. Let’s **go swimming**. (*or* Let’s **go for a swim**.) ‖ Trời đẹp. **Đi bơi** đi.
- Richard has a small boat and he often **goes sailing**. ‖ Richard có một chiếc thuyền nhỏ và anh ấy hay **đi thuyền buồm**.
- I **went jogging** before breakfast this morning. ‖ Sáng nay tôi **chạy bộ** trước bữa sáng.
@rules
A: go to + place/event (go to work / go to China / go to bed / go to sleep); but go home (no to). ‖ go to + nơi chốn/sự kiện (go to work / go to China / go to bed / go to sleep); nhưng go home (không có to).
B: go on holiday / a trip / a tour / a cruise / strike. ‖ go on holiday / a trip / a tour / a cruise / strike.
C: go for a walk / a run / a swim / a drink / a meal / coffee. ‖ go for a walk / a run / a swim / a drink / a meal / coffee.
D: go + -ing for sports and shopping (go swimming / go skiing / go shopping). ‖ go + -ing cho thể thao và mua sắm (go swimming / go skiing / go shopping).
