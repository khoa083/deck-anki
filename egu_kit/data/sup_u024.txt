@rules
E149: a/an when mentioned for the first time; the when it is clear which one (already mentioned, the only one, east end, middle of); some + plural noun. ‖ a/an khi nhắc lần đầu; the khi rõ là cái nào (đã nhắc, duy nhất, east end, middle of); some + danh từ số nhiều.
E151: a/an for one of many (an old car, a doctor, a car which is reliable, a test drive); the for a specific one (the first owner, the city centre, the hospital in Hills Road, the fastest, the battery); five days a week. ‖ a/an cho một trong nhiều (an old car, a doctor, a car which is reliable, a test drive); the cho cái cụ thể (the first owner, the city centre, the hospital in Hills Road, the fastest, the battery); five days a week.
E152: the + family name (the Campbells), the + specific group, the east coast, the rush hour, the school bus, the road, the + gallery, the + river, the city centre; no the with general uncountable nouns (paper, noise), and in go to school, by bus, at work, at art school, away from home. ‖ the + họ (the Campbells), the + nhóm cụ thể, the east coast, the rush hour, the school bus, the road, the + phòng tranh, the + sông, the city centre; không dùng the với danh từ không đếm được nói chung (paper, noise), và trong go to school, by bus, at work, at art school, away from home.
E154: No the with names of most countries (Singapore, Malaysia, Spain), people’s names (King Felipe), squares, castles, general abstract nouns (global warming, information) and people in general (businessmen); the with plural or ‘Kingdom’ names (the Philippines, the United Kingdom), the Queen, galleries (the National Gallery). ‖ Không dùng the với tên hầu hết quốc gia (Singapore, Malaysia, Spain), tên người (King Felipe), quảng trường, lâu đài, danh từ trừu tượng nói chung (global warming, information) và người nói chung (businessmen); dùng the với tên số nhiều hoặc có ‘Kingdom’ (the Philippines, the United Kingdom), the Queen, phòng tranh (the National Gallery).
