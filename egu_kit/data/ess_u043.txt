@title_vi: isn’t, haven’t, don’t v.v. (câu phủ định)
@theory
## A | not (n’t) in negative sentences | not (n’t) trong câu phủ định
We use **not** (**n’t**) in negative sentences: ‖ Dùng **not** (**n’t**) trong câu phủ định:
|! positive ‖ khẳng định | negative ‖ phủ định | example ‖ ví dụ
| am | **am not** (**’m not**) | I**’m not** tired.
| is | **is not** (**isn’t** or **’s not**) | It **isn’t** (or It**’s not**) raining.
| are | **are not** (**aren’t** or **’re not**) | They **aren’t** (or They**’re not**) here.
| was | **was not** (**wasn’t**) | Julian **wasn’t** hungry.
| were | **were not** (**weren’t**) | The shops **weren’t** open.
| have | **have not** (**haven’t**) | I **haven’t** finished my work.
| has | **has not** (**hasn’t**) | Sue **hasn’t** got a car.
| will | **will not** (**won’t**) | We **won’t** be here tomorrow.
| can | **cannot** (**can’t**) | George **can’t** drive.
| could | **could not** (**couldn’t**) | I **couldn’t** sleep last night.
| must | **must not** (**mustn’t**) | I **mustn’t** forget to phone Jane.
| should | **should not** (**shouldn’t**) | You **shouldn’t** work so hard.
| would | **would not** (**wouldn’t**) | I **wouldn’t** like to be an actor.
## B | don’t / doesn’t / didn’t | don’t / doesn’t / didn’t
Present simple negative: I / we / you / they **do not** (**don’t**), he / she / it **does not** (**doesn’t**) + work / live / go etc. Past simple negative: I / they / he / she etc. **did not** (**didn’t**). ‖ Phủ định hiện tại đơn: I / we / you / they **do not** (**don’t**), he / she / it **does not** (**doesn’t**) + work / live / go v.v. Phủ định quá khứ đơn: I / they / he / she v.v. **did not** (**didn’t**).
- I **want** to go out. → I **don’t want** to go out. ‖ Tôi **muốn** ra ngoài. → Tôi **không muốn** ra ngoài.
- They **work** hard. → They **don’t work** hard. ‖ Họ **làm việc** chăm chỉ. → Họ **không làm việc** chăm chỉ.
- Lisa **plays** the guitar. → Lisa **doesn’t play** the guitar. ‖ Lisa **chơi** ghi-ta. → Lisa **không chơi** ghi-ta.
- My father **likes** his job. → My father **doesn’t like** his job. ‖ Bố tôi **thích** công việc của mình. → Bố tôi **không thích** công việc của mình.
- I **got** up early this morning. → I **didn’t get** up early this morning. ‖ Sáng nay tôi **dậy** sớm. → Sáng nay tôi **không dậy** sớm.
- They **worked** hard yesterday. → They **didn’t work** hard yesterday. ‖ Hôm qua họ **làm việc** chăm chỉ. → Hôm qua họ **không làm việc** chăm chỉ.
- We **played** tennis. → We **didn’t play** tennis. ‖ Chúng tôi **chơi** quần vợt. → Chúng tôi **không chơi** quần vợt.
- Emily **had** dinner with us. → Emily **didn’t have** dinner with us. ‖ Emily **ăn tối** với chúng tôi. → Emily **không ăn tối** với chúng tôi.
## C | Don’t … (imperative) and do as the main verb | Don’t … (mệnh lệnh) và do là động từ chính
- **Look!** → **Don’t look!** / **Wait** for me. → **Don’t wait** for me. ‖ **Nhìn kìa!** → **Đừng nhìn!** / **Đợi** tôi. → **Đừng đợi** tôi.
Sometimes **do** is the main verb (**don’t do** / **doesn’t do** / **didn’t do**): ‖ Đôi khi **do** là động từ chính (**don’t do** / **doesn’t do** / **didn’t do**):
- **Do** something! → **Don’t do** anything! ‖ **Làm** gì đi chứ! → **Đừng làm** gì cả!
- Sue **does** a lot at weekends. → Sue **doesn’t do** much at weekends. ‖ Cuối tuần Sue **làm** nhiều việc. → Cuối tuần Sue **không làm** gì nhiều.
- I **did** what you said. → I **didn’t do** what you said. ‖ Tôi **đã làm** như bạn bảo. → Tôi **không làm** như bạn bảo.
@rules
A: Negative = auxiliary/modal + not (n’t): isn’t, aren’t, wasn’t, haven’t, won’t, can’t, couldn’t, shouldn’t … ‖ Phủ định = trợ động từ/động từ khuyết thiếu + not (n’t): isn’t, aren’t, wasn’t, haven’t, won’t, can’t, couldn’t, shouldn’t …
B: Present/past simple: don’t / doesn’t / didn’t + infinitive. ‖ Hiện tại/quá khứ đơn: don’t / doesn’t / didn’t + nguyên mẫu.
C: Imperative negative: Don’t + infinitive; do as main verb: don’t do / didn’t do. ‖ Mệnh lệnh phủ định: Don’t + nguyên mẫu; do là động từ chính: don’t do / didn’t do.
