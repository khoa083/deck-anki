@title_vi: to … (I want to do) và -ing (I enjoy doing)
@theory
## A | verbs + to … (I want to do) | động từ + to … (I want to do)
**want, plan, decide, try, hope, expect, offer, forget, need, promise, refuse, learn** + **to** … (**to do** / **to work** / **to be** etc.) ‖ **want, plan, decide, try, hope, expect, offer, forget, need, promise, refuse, learn** + **to** … (**to do** / **to work** / **to be** v.v.)
- What do you **want to do** this evening? ‖ Tối nay bạn **muốn làm** gì?
- It’s not very late. We don’t **need to go** home yet. ‖ Chưa muộn lắm. Chúng ta **chưa cần về** nhà.
- Tina has **decided to sell** her car. ‖ Tina đã **quyết định bán** xe.
- You **forgot to switch** off the light when you went out. ‖ Bạn **quên tắt** đèn khi ra ngoài.
- My brother is **learning to drive**. ‖ Anh tôi đang **học lái** xe.
- I **tried to read** my book, but I was too tired. ‖ Tôi **cố đọc** sách, nhưng tôi mệt quá.
## B | verbs + -ing (I enjoy doing) | động từ + -ing (I enjoy doing)
**enjoy, stop, mind, finish, suggest** + **-ing** (**doing** / **working** / **being** etc.) ‖ **enjoy, stop, mind, finish, suggest** + **-ing** (**doing** / **working** / **being** v.v.)
- I **enjoy dancing**. (*not* enjoy to dance) ‖ Tôi **thích nhảy**. (*không nói* enjoy to dance)
- I don’t **mind getting** up early. ‖ Tôi không **ngại dậy** sớm.
- Has it **stopped raining**? ‖ Trời **tạnh mưa** chưa?
- Sonia **suggested going** to the cinema. ‖ Sonia **gợi ý đi** xem phim.
## C | verbs + -ing or to … | động từ + -ing hoặc to …
**like, love, prefer, hate, start, begin, continue** + **-ing** (**doing** etc.) *or* **to** … (**to do** etc.) ‖ **like, love, prefer, hate, start, begin, continue** + **-ing** (**doing** v.v.) *hoặc* **to** … (**to do** v.v.)
- Do you **like getting** up early? *or* Do you **like to get** up early? ‖ Bạn có **thích dậy** sớm không?
- I **prefer travelling** by car. *or* I **prefer to travel** by car. ‖ Tôi **thích đi** bằng ô tô hơn.
- Anna **loves dancing**. *or* Anna **loves to dance**. ‖ Anna **rất thích nhảy**.
- I **hate being** late. *or* I **hate to be** late. ‖ Tôi **ghét** bị muộn.
- It **started raining**. *or* It **started to rain**. ‖ Trời **bắt đầu mưa**.
## D | would like to … etc. | would like to … v.v.
**would like, would love, would prefer, would hate** + **to** … (**to do** / **to work** / **to be** etc.) ‖ **would like, would love, would prefer, would hate** + **to** … (**to do** / **to work** / **to be** v.v.)
- Amy **would like to meet** you. ‖ Amy **muốn gặp** bạn.
- I**’d love to go** to Australia. (I**’d** = I **would**) ‖ Tôi **rất muốn đi** Úc.
- ‘**Would** you **like to sit** down?’ ‘No, I**’d prefer to stand**, thank you.’ ‖ ‘Bạn có **muốn ngồi** không?’ ‘Không, tôi **muốn đứng** hơn, cảm ơn.’
- I like this apartment. I **wouldn’t like to move**. ‖ Tôi thích căn hộ này. Tôi **không muốn chuyển** đi.
- I live in a small village. I**’d hate to live** in a big city. ‖ Tôi sống ở một ngôi làng nhỏ. Tôi **sẽ rất ghét sống** ở thành phố lớn.
@rules
A: want/plan/decide/try/hope/expect/offer/forget/need/promise/refuse/learn + to … ‖ want/plan/decide/try/hope/expect/offer/forget/need/promise/refuse/learn + to …
B: enjoy/stop/mind/finish/suggest + -ing (not enjoy to dance). ‖ enjoy/stop/mind/finish/suggest + -ing (không nói enjoy to dance).
C: like/love/prefer/hate/start/begin/continue + -ing or to … ‖ like/love/prefer/hate/start/begin/continue + -ing hoặc to …
D: would like/love/prefer/hate + to … ‖ would like/love/prefer/hate + to …
