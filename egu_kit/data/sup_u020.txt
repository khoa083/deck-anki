@rules
E128: Reported questions: asked / wanted to know / wondered + question word or if / whether + subject + verb (statement word order, tense moves back, no do / did). ‖ Câu hỏi tường thuật: asked / wanted to know / wondered + từ để hỏi hoặc if / whether + chủ ngữ + động từ (trật tự câu kể, lùi thì, không dùng do / did).
E129: tell somebody (something), tell the truth / a joke / lies; say something (to somebody), say anything / what to say. ‖ tell + người (+ điều gì), tell the truth / a joke / lies; say + điều gì (+ to + người), say anything / what to say.
E130: tell + person (told him all about …, told him that …); say (that) … / say to somebody; asked me to tell him. ‖ tell + người (told him all about …, told him that …); say (that) … / say to + người; asked me to tell him.
E131: tell needs a person object (told us that …); say has no person object (said that …); ask somebody not to do; past tense may stay unchanged for facts still true. ‖ tell cần tân ngữ chỉ người (told us that …); say không có tân ngữ chỉ người (said that …); ask somebody not to do; thì có thể giữ nguyên khi sự việc vẫn đúng.

E128 | ‘What will you do when you leave school?’ asked Jennifer. → Jennifer asked me ___ when I left school. | what I would do | what would I do | what I would did | ‘Ra trường bạn sẽ làm gì?’ Jennifer hỏi. → Jennifer hỏi tôi [sẽ làm gì] khi ra trường. | will → would; S + V.
E128 | ‘How did you know my name?’ the journalist asked the security guard. → The journalist wanted to know ___. | how the security guard knew her name | how did the security guard know her name | how the security guard did know her name | ‘Sao anh biết tên tôi?’ nhà báo hỏi bảo vệ. → Nhà báo muốn biết [bảo vệ làm sao biết tên cô]. | Không dùng did trong câu hỏi tường thuật.

E128 | ‘Have you seen the blue memory stick?’ Bernie asked his wife. → Bernie wondered ___ the blue memory stick. | whether his wife had seen | had his wife seen | whether had his wife seen | ‘Em có thấy cái USB xanh không?’ Bernie hỏi vợ. → Bernie tự hỏi [vợ có thấy] cái USB xanh không. | whether + S + had + V3.
E128 | ‘Why didn’t Isobel phone me?’ asked her brother. → Isobel’s brother wanted to know ___. | why she hadn’t phoned him | why hadn’t she phoned him | why she didn’t phoned him | ‘Sao Isobel không gọi cho anh?’ anh trai cô hỏi. → Anh trai Isobel muốn biết [sao cô không gọi cho anh]. | didn’t phone → hadn’t phoned; S + V.
E128 | ‘Will you carry my laptop for me please, Rosie?’ Richard asked. → Richard asked Rosie ___ his laptop for him. | whether she would carry | would she carry | whether she will carried | ‘Rosie, em xách laptop giúp anh nhé?’ Richard hỏi. → Richard hỏi Rosie [liệu cô có xách] laptop giúp anh không. | will → would; whether + S + V.
E128 | ‘When can I see the doctor?’ Charles asked the receptionist. → Charles asked the receptionist ___ the doctor. | when he could see | when could he see | when he can to see | ‘Khi nào tôi gặp được bác sĩ?’ Charles hỏi lễ tân. → Charles hỏi lễ tân [khi nào anh gặp được] bác sĩ. | can → could; S + V.

E129 | What ___ to Wendy last night? She looks terribly upset this morning. | did you say | did you tell | did you said | Tối qua [bạn đã nói] gì với Wendy? Sáng nay trông cô ấy buồn kinh khủng. | say something to somebody.
E129 | Is something wrong? Can you ___ me about it? | tell | say | told | Có chuyện gì à? Bạn [kể] cho tôi nghe được không? | tell + người + about.
