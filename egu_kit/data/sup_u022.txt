E138: Preposition + -ing: begin by doing, there’s no point in doing, without doing, (use it) for doing, before doing, waste time doing, encourage yourself by doing. ‖ Giới từ + -ing: begin by doing, there’s no point in doing, without doing, (use it) for doing, before doing, waste time doing, encourage yourself by doing.
E139: by doing (how), there’s no point (in) doing, waste time doing (no preposition), avoid … by doing, it’s (not) worth doing. ‖ by doing (cách thức), there’s no point (in) doing, waste time doing (không giới từ), avoid … by doing, it’s (not) worth doing.
E141: used to + base form = a past habit or state; be / get used to + -ing = be / become accustomed to. ‖ used to + nguyên mẫu = thói quen hoặc trạng thái trong quá khứ; be / get used to + -ing = quen với.
E142: dream of / about doing, afraid to do (too nervous to try) / afraid of doing (worried something will happen), succeed in doing, look forward to doing, sorry to do (now) / sorry for doing (past), fail to do, think of doing, interested in doing, allowed to do, prevent somebody from doing, the only one to do, likely to do. ‖ dream of / about doing, afraid to do (sợ không dám làm) / afraid of doing (sợ điều gì xảy ra), succeed in doing, look forward to doing, sorry to do (hiện tại) / sorry for doing (đã làm), fail to do, think of doing, interested in doing, allowed to do, prevent ai đó from doing, the only one to do, likely to do.
E143: Past simple for events in sequence; past continuous for actions in progress; past perfect for earlier actions; present simple for habits and facts; -ing / to … after verbs. ‖ Quá khứ đơn cho chuỗi sự kiện; quá khứ tiếp diễn cho hành động đang diễn ra; quá khứ hoàn thành cho việc xảy ra trước; hiện tại đơn cho thói quen và sự thật; -ing / to … sau động từ.
