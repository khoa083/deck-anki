@title_vi: Do you know where … ? / I don’t know what … v.v. (câu hỏi gián tiếp)
@theory
## A | Do you know where Paula is? | Do you know where Paula is? (bạn có biết Paula ở đâu không?)
We say: Where **is Paula**? but **Do you know** where **Paula is**? (*not* Do you know where is Paula?) ‖ Ta nói: Where **is Paula**? nhưng **Do you know** where **Paula is**? (*không nói* Do you know where is Paula?)
In the same way we say: **I know** / **I don’t know** / **Can you tell me** where **Paula is**. ‖ Tương tự: **I know** / **I don’t know** / **Can you tell me** where **Paula is**.
|! question ‖ câu hỏi | Do you know / Can you tell me … ? / I know / I don’t know / I don’t remember …
| Who **are those people**? | who **those people are**
| How old **is Nicola**? | how old **Nicola is**
| What time **is it**? | what time **it is**
| Where **can I** go? | where **I can** go
| How much **is this camera**? | how much **this camera is**
| When **are you** going away? | when **you’re** going away
| Where **have they** gone? | where **they have** gone
| What **was Kate** wearing? | what **Kate was** wearing
## B | Questions with do/does/did | Câu hỏi có do/does/did
Where **does he live**? but **Do you know** where **he lives**? (*not* Do you know where does he live?) ‖ Where **does he live**? nhưng **Do you know** where **he lives**? (*không nói* Do you know where does he live?)
|! question ‖ câu hỏi | Do you know … ? / I know / I don’t know / I don’t remember …
| How **do airplanes** fly? | how **airplanes fly**
| What **does Jane** want? | what **Jane wants**
| Why **did she** go home? | why **she went** home
| Where **did I** put the key? | where **I put** the key
## C | Is … ? / Do … ? / Can … ? (yes/no questions) → if / whether | Is … ? / Do … ? / Can … ? (câu hỏi có/không) → if / whether
|! question ‖ câu hỏi | Do you know if (whether) … ? / I don’t know if (whether) …
| **Is Jack** at home? | **Jack is** at home
| **Have they** got a car? | **they’ve got** a car
| **Can Ben** swim? | **Ben can** swim
| **Do they** live near here? | **they live** near here
| **Did anybody** see you? | **anybody saw** you
You can use **if** or **whether** in these sentences: ‖ Có thể dùng **if** hoặc **whether** trong các câu này:
- Do you know **if** they’ve got a car? *or* Do you know **whether** they’ve got a car? ‖ Bạn có biết **liệu** họ có xe hơi **không**?
- I don’t know **if** anybody saw me. *or* I don’t know **whether** anybody saw me. ‖ Tôi không biết **liệu** có ai thấy tôi **không**.
@rules
A: After Do you know / Can you tell me / I don’t know …: question word + subject + verb (where Paula is, not where is Paula). ‖ Sau Do you know / Can you tell me / I don’t know …: từ để hỏi + chủ ngữ + động từ (where Paula is, không nói where is Paula).
B: No do/does/did in these clauses: where he lives (not where does he live), why she went (not why did she go). ‖ Không dùng do/does/did trong mệnh đề này: where he lives (không nói where does he live), why she went (không nói why did she go).
C: Yes/no questions → if / whether + subject + verb (Do you know if they’ve got a car?). ‖ Câu hỏi có/không → if / whether + chủ ngữ + động từ (Do you know if they’ve got a car?).
D: In a direct question, use normal question word order. ‖ Trong câu hỏi trực tiếp, dùng trật tự câu hỏi bình thường.
