@title_vi: there was/were / there has/have been / there will be (đã có / sẽ có)
@theory
## A | there was / there were (past) | there was / there were (quá khứ)
**There is** a train every hour. The time now is 11.15. **There was** a train at 11 o’clock. ‖ Cứ mỗi giờ **có** một chuyến tàu. Bây giờ là 11 giờ 15. Lúc 11 giờ **đã có** một chuyến.
|! there is / are (present) ‖ hiện tại | there was / were (past) ‖ quá khứ
| **There is** nothing on TV tonight. | **There was** nothing on TV last night.
| We are staying at a very big hotel. **There are** 550 rooms. | We stayed at a very big hotel. **There were** 550 rooms.
| Is everything OK? **Are there** any problems? | Was everything OK yesterday? **Were there** any problems?
| I’m hungry, but **there isn’t** anything to eat. | I was hungry when I got home, but **there wasn’t** anything to eat.
## B | there has been / there have been (present perfect) | there has been / there have been (hiện tại hoàn thành)
- Look! **There’s been** an accident. (**there’s been** = there **has** been) ‖ Nhìn kìa! **Có** tai nạn **rồi**. (**there’s been** = there **has** been)
- This road is very dangerous. **There have been** many accidents. ‖ Con đường này rất nguy hiểm. **Đã có** nhiều vụ tai nạn.
Compare **there was** (past): ‖ So sánh **there was** (quá khứ):
- **There was** an accident **last night**. (*not* There has been an accident last night.) ‖ **Tối qua có** một vụ tai nạn. (*không nói* There has been an accident last night.)
## C | there will be | there will be (sẽ có)
- Do you think **there will be** a lot of people at the party on Saturday? ‖ Bạn có nghĩ thứ Bảy **sẽ có** nhiều người đến bữa tiệc không?
- The manager of the company is leaving, so **there will be** a new manager soon. ‖ Giám đốc công ty sắp nghỉ, nên chẳng bao lâu nữa **sẽ có** giám đốc mới.
- I’m going away tomorrow. I’ll do my packing today because **there won’t be** time tomorrow. (**there won’t be** = there **will not** be) ‖ Mai tôi đi xa. Hôm nay tôi sẽ xếp đồ vì mai **sẽ không có** thời gian.
- **There will be** rain tomorrow afternoon. ‖ Chiều mai **sẽ có** mưa.
@rules
A: there was + singular / there were + plural (past); was there / were there …?; there wasn’t / weren’t. ‖ there was + số ít / there were + số nhiều (quá khứ); was there / were there …?; there wasn’t / weren’t.
B: there has been / there have been (present perfect, result now / until now); with a finished time use there was. ‖ there has been / there have been (hiện tại hoàn thành); với thời gian đã qua dùng there was.
C: there will be / there won’t be (future). ‖ there will be / there won’t be (tương lai).
P: there is / there are for the present. ‖ there is / there are cho hiện tại.
