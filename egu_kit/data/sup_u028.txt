@rules
E169: verb + object together (left the meeting early); place before time (in the park in summer); give somebody something; mid-position adverbs (also, all, definitely, even) go before the main verb or after be / the first auxiliary. ‖ động từ + tân ngữ đi liền (left the meeting early); nơi chốn trước thời gian (in the park in summer); give somebody something; trạng từ giữa câu (also, all, definitely, even) đứng trước động từ chính hoặc sau be / trợ động từ đầu tiên.
E170: Frequency adverbs go before the main verb, after be, after the first auxiliary, and after don’t / doesn’t (doesn’t usually eat). ‖ Trạng từ tần suất đứng trước động từ chính, sau be, sau trợ động từ đầu tiên và sau don’t / doesn’t (doesn’t usually eat).
E171: always / even / probably / all / both / certainly in mid position: after be, after the first auxiliary, before the main verb; probably / certainly before a negative auxiliary (I certainly didn’t). ‖ always / even / probably / all / both / certainly ở giữa câu: sau be, sau trợ động từ đầu tiên, trước động từ chính; probably / certainly đứng trước trợ động từ phủ định (I certainly didn’t).
E172: usually / probably / even / never / often / almost in mid position (doesn’t even belong, has never been, often has, has almost finished). ‖ usually / probably / even / never / often / almost ở giữa câu (doesn’t even belong, has never been, often has, has almost finished).
