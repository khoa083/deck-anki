@title_vi: this/that/these/those (này / kia)
@theory
## A | this / that / these / those | this / that / these / those
|! | here ‖ ở đây (gần) | there ‖ ở kia (xa)
| singular ‖ số ít | **this** picture (= this picture here) | **that** picture (= that picture there)
| plural ‖ số nhiều | **these** flowers (= these flowers here) | **those** people (= those people there)
- Do you like **this** picture? / Do you like **that** picture? ‖ Bạn có thích bức tranh **này** không? / Bạn có thích bức tranh **kia** không?
- **These** flowers are for you. / Who are **those** people? ‖ **Những bông hoa này** dành cho bạn. / **Những người kia** là ai?
## B | with or without a noun | có hoặc không có danh từ đi kèm
We use **this / that / these / those** with a noun (**this picture** / **those girls** etc.) or without a noun: ‖ Dùng **this / that / these / those** với danh từ (**this picture** / **those girls** v.v.) hoặc không có danh từ:
|! with a noun ‖ có danh từ | without a noun ‖ không có danh từ
| **This hotel** is expensive, but it’s very nice. | **This** is a nice hotel, but it’s very expensive.
| ‘Who’s **that girl**?’ ‘I don’t know.’ | ‘Excuse me, is **this** your bag?’ ‘Oh yes, thank you.’
| Do you like **these shoes**? I bought them last week. | Who’s **that**? (= Who is that person?)
| **Those apples** look nice. Can I have one? | Which shoes do you prefer – **these** or **those**?
## C | that = something that has happened / what somebody has just said | that = việc vừa xảy ra / điều ai đó vừa nói
**that** = something that has happened: ‖ **that** = việc vừa xảy ra:
- ‘I’m sorry I forgot to phone you.’ ‘**That**’s all right.’ ‖ ‘Xin lỗi tôi quên gọi cho bạn.’ ‘**Không sao** đâu.’
- **That** was a really nice meal. Thank you very much. ‖ **Đó** là một bữa ăn rất ngon. Cảm ơn bạn nhiều.
**that** = what somebody has just said: ‖ **that** = điều ai đó vừa nói:
- ‘You’re a teacher, aren’t you?’ ‘Yes, **that**’s right.’ ‖ ‘Bạn là giáo viên phải không?’ ‘Vâng, **đúng vậy**.’
- ‘Martin has a new job.’ ‘Really? I didn’t know **that**.’ ‖ ‘Martin có việc mới rồi.’ ‘Thật à? Tôi không biết **chuyện đó**.’
- ‘I’m going on holiday next week.’ ‘Oh, **that**’s nice.’ ‖ ‘Tuần sau tôi đi nghỉ.’ ‘Ồ, **hay quá**.’
## D | this is … / is that … ? on the phone; this is … to introduce people | this is … / is that … ? khi gọi điện; this is … để giới thiệu
- (on the phone) Hi Sarah, **this** is David. (**this** = the speaker) / Is **that** Sarah? (**that** = the other person) ‖ (điện thoại) Chào Sarah, **tôi là** David. (**this** = người nói) / **Có phải** Sarah **không**? (**that** = người bên kia)
- A: Ben, **this is** Chris. B: Hello, Chris – nice to meet you. C: Hi. ‖ A: Ben, **đây là** Chris. B: Chào Chris – rất vui được gặp bạn. C: Chào.
@rules
A: this (singular) / these (plural) = here, near; that (singular) / those (plural) = there, further away. ‖ this (số ít) / these (số nhiều) = ở đây, gần; that (số ít) / those (số nhiều) = ở kia, xa.
B: this/that/these/those can be used with or without a noun. ‖ this/that/these/those có thể dùng có hoặc không có danh từ.
C: that = something that has happened or what somebody has just said (That’s all right / That’s right). ‖ that = việc vừa xảy ra hoặc điều ai đó vừa nói (That’s all right / That’s right).
D: On the phone: this is … (me) / is that …? (you); introducing: this is … ‖ Điện thoại: this is … (tôi) / is that …? (bạn); giới thiệu: this is …
