@title_vi: not as … as (không … bằng)
@theory
## A | not as … as | not as … as (không … bằng)
- ‘I’m 93.’ ‘I’m 96.’ She’s old, but she’s **not as old as** he is. ‖ ‘Tôi 93 tuổi.’ ‘Tôi 96 tuổi.’ Bà ấy già, nhưng **không già bằng** ông ấy.
- Box A is**n’t as big as** Box B. ‖ Hộp A **không lớn bằng** hộp B.
- Rome **is not as old as** Athens. (= Athens is **older**) ‖ Rome **không cổ bằng** Athens. (= Athens **cổ hơn**)
- The Grand Hotel **isn’t as expensive as** the Europa. (= the Europa is **more expensive**) ‖ Khách sạn Grand **không đắt bằng** khách sạn Europa.
- I **don’t** play tennis **as often as** you. (= you play **more often**) ‖ Tôi **không** chơi quần vợt **thường xuyên bằng** bạn.
- The weather is better than it was yesterday. It **isn’t as cold**. (= as cold **as it was yesterday**) ‖ Thời tiết đẹp hơn hôm qua. **Không lạnh bằng** hôm qua.
## B | not as much as … / not as many as … | not as much as … / not as many as …
- I don’t have **as much money as** you. (= you’ve got **more money**) ‖ Tôi không có **nhiều tiền bằng** bạn.
- I don’t know **as many people as** you. (= you know **more people**) ‖ Tôi không quen **nhiều người bằng** bạn.
- I don’t go out **as much as** you. (= you go out **more**) ‖ Tôi không ra ngoài **nhiều bằng** bạn.
## C | not as … as and than | not as … as và than
|! not as … as | than
| Rome is **not as old as** Athens. | Athens is **older than** Rome. (*not* older as Rome)
| Tennis **isn’t as popular as** football. | Football is **more popular than** tennis.
| I **don’t** go out **as much as** you. | You go out **more than** me.
We usually say: as **me** / as **him** / as **her** etc. You can say: ‖ Ta thường nói: as **me** / as **him** / as **her** v.v. Có thể nói:
- She’s not as old **as him**. *or* She’s not as old **as he is**. ‖ Bà ấy không già **bằng ông ấy**.
- You don’t work as hard **as me**. *or* You don’t work as hard **as I do**. ‖ Bạn không làm việc chăm **bằng tôi**.
## D | the same as … | the same as … (giống như)
- The weather today is **the same as** yesterday. ‖ Thời tiết hôm nay **giống như** hôm qua.
- My hair is **the same colour as** yours. ‖ Tóc tôi **cùng màu với** tóc bạn.
- I arrived at **the same time as** Tom. ‖ Tôi đến **cùng lúc với** Tom.
@rules
A: not as + adjective/adverb + as = less than (Rome is not as old as Athens). ‖ not as + tính từ/trạng từ + as = không … bằng (Rome is not as old as Athens).
B: not as much + uncountable / not as many + plural + as. ‖ not as much + không đếm được / not as many + số nhiều + as.
C: as … as but comparative + than (older than, not older as); as me / as I am. ‖ as … as nhưng so sánh hơn + than (older than, không nói older as); as me / as I am.
D: the same as … / the same + noun + as … ‖ the same as … / the same + danh từ + as …
