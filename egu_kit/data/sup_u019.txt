@rules
E121: Reported → direct speech: was / were → am / is / are; had done → have done or did; could → can; the week before → last week; the next day → tomorrow; she / they → I / we. ‖ Tường thuật → lời nói trực tiếp: was / were → am / is / are; had done → have done hoặc did; could → can; the week before → last week; the next day → tomorrow; she / they → I / we.
E122: In reported speech the verb usually moves back: did / have done → had done; think → thought; is → was. ‖ Trong câu tường thuật động từ thường lùi thì: did / have done → had done; think → thought; is → was.
E123: Reported speech after said / told / asked: is → was, came → had come, am doing → was doing, am going to → was going to, want → wanted, have → had; told somebody to do. ‖ Câu tường thuật sau said / told / asked: is → was, came → had come, am doing → was doing, am going to → was going to, want → wanted, have → had; told somebody to do.
E124: Reported statements and questions: she said she was / wasn’t …, asked her if she would …, said she was going to …. ‖ Câu kể và câu hỏi tường thuật: she said she was / wasn’t …, asked her if she would …, said she was going to ….
E125: You said (that) there was / we would / it could be …: present → past, will → would, can → could, has been done → had been done. ‖ You said (that) there was / we would / it could be …: hiện tại → quá khứ, will → would, can → could, has been done → had been done.
E126: Reported → direct questions: asked where I came from → Where do you come from?; asked whether I was going → Are you going …?; asked if we could → Can we / they …? ‖ Câu hỏi tường thuật → trực tiếp: asked where I came from → Where do you come from?; asked whether I was going → Are you going …?; asked if we could → Can we / they …?
