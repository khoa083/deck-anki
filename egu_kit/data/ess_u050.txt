@title_vi: She said that … / He told me that … (câu tường thuật)
@theory
## A | Reporting what people said | Thuật lại lời người khác nói
Last week you went to a party. A lot of your friends were there. Today you meet Paul. You tell him what your friends said: ‖ Tuần trước bạn đi dự tiệc. Nhiều bạn bè của bạn có ở đó. Hôm nay bạn gặp Paul. Bạn kể cho anh ấy những gì bạn bè đã nói:
|! present → past ‖ hiện tại → quá khứ | what they said ‖ lời nói | reported ‖ tường thuật
| **am / is** → **was** | Clare: ‘**I’m** enjoying my new job. **My father isn’t** well.’ | Clare said that **she was** enjoying her new job. She said that **her father wasn’t** well.
| **are** → **were** | Sarah and Ben: ‘**We’re** going to buy a house.’ | Sarah and Ben said that **they were** going to buy a house.
| **have / has** → **had** | Peter: ‘**I have** to leave early. **My sister has** gone to Australia.’ | Peter said that **he had** to leave early. He said that **his sister had** gone to Australia.
| **can** → **could** | Kate: ‘**I can’t** find a job.’ | Kate said that **she couldn’t** find a job.
| **will** → **would** | Steve: ‘**I’ll** phone you.’ | Steve said that **he would** phone me.
| **do / does** → **did** | Rachel: ‘**I don’t** like my job. **My son doesn’t** like school.’ | Rachel said that **she didn’t** like her job. She said that **her son didn’t** like school.
| **look** → **looked**, **feel** → **felt** | Mike: ‘**You look** tired.’ You: ‘**I feel** fine.’ | Mike said that **I looked** tired. I said that **I felt** fine.
## B | say and tell | say và tell
|! say (→ said) | tell (→ told)
| He **said** that he was tired. (*not* He said me) | He **told me** that he was tired. (*not* He told that)
| What did she **say to** you? (*not* say you) | What did she **tell you**? (*not* tell to you)
We say **he told me**, **I told Ann** etc. (*not* ‘he told to me’, ‘I told to Ann’). We say **he said to me**, **I said to Ann** etc. (*not* ‘he said me’, ‘I said Ann’). ‖ Ta nói **he told me**, **I told Ann** v.v. (*không nói* ‘he told to me’, ‘I told to Ann’). Ta nói **he said to me**, **I said to Ann** v.v. (*không nói* ‘he said me’, ‘I said Ann’).
You can say: He said **that** he was tired. *or* He said he was tired. (without **that**) ‖ Có thể nói: He said **that** he was tired. *hoặc* He said he was tired. (bỏ **that**)
- Kate told me **that** she couldn’t find a job. *or* Kate told me she couldn’t find a job. ‖ Kate nói với tôi **rằng** cô ấy không tìm được việc.
@rules
A: Reported speech: present → past (am/is → was, are → were, have/has → had, can → could, will → would, do/does → did, look → looked). ‖ Câu tường thuật: hiện tại → quá khứ (am/is → was, are → were, have/has → had, can → could, will → would, do/does → did, look → looked).
B1: say (said) + that … / say to somebody – not ‘said me’. ‖ say (said) + that … / say to somebody – không nói ‘said me’.
B2: tell (told) + person + that … – not ‘told that’, not ‘told to me’. ‖ tell (told) + người + that … – không nói ‘told that’, không nói ‘told to me’.
