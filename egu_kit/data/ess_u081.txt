@title_vi: all / most / some / any / no/none (tất cả / hầu hết / một số / không …)
@theory
## A | children / money / books (in general) and the children / the money / these books | children / money / books (nói chung) và the children / the money / these books
|! in general ‖ nói chung | specific ‖ cụ thể
| **Children** like playing. (= children in general) | Where are **the children**? (= our children)
| **Money** isn’t everything. (= money in general) | I want to buy a car, but I don’t have **the money**. (= the money for a car)
| I enjoy reading **books**. | Have you read **these books**?
| Everybody needs **friends**. | I often go out with **my friends**.
## B | most / most of … , some / some of … etc. | most / most of … , some / some of … v.v.
|! most / some etc. + noun ‖ + danh từ | most of / some of etc. + the / this / my … ‖ + the / this / my …
| **All cities** have the same problems. (= cities in general) | **All (of) the students in our class** passed the exam.
| **Most children** like playing. (= children in general) | **Most of the children at this school** are under 11 years old.
| **Some books** are better than others. | **Some of these books** are very old.
| I don’t want **any money**. | I don’t want **any of this money**.
| He has **no friends**. | **None of my friends** live near me.
You can say **all the** … or **all of the** … (with or without **of**): ‖ Có thể nói **all the** … hoặc **all of the** … (có hoặc không có **of**):
- **All the students in our class** passed the exam. (*or* **All of the students** …) ‖ **Tất cả học sinh lớp chúng tôi** đều đỗ.
- Amy has lived in London **all her life**. (*or* … **all of her life**.) ‖ Amy đã sống ở London **cả đời**.
Do not use **of** in these sentences: ‖ Không dùng **of** trong những câu này:
- **Most people** drive too fast. (*not* Most of people) ‖ **Hầu hết mọi người** lái xe quá nhanh. (*không nói* Most of people)
- **Some birds** can’t fly. (*not* Some of birds) ‖ **Một số loài chim** không biết bay. (*không nói* Some of birds)
## C | all of it / most of them / none of us etc. | all of it / most of them / none of us v.v.
**all / most / some / any / none** + **of** + **it / them / us / you** ‖ **all / most / some / any / none** + **of** + **it / them / us / you**
- You can have **some of this cake**, but not **all of it**. ‖ Bạn có thể ăn **một ít bánh này**, nhưng không phải **tất cả**.
- A: Do you know those people? B: **Most of them**, but not **all of them**. ‖ A: Bạn có quen những người kia không? B: **Hầu hết**, nhưng không phải **tất cả**.
- **Some of us** are going out tonight. Why don’t you come with us? ‖ **Vài người trong chúng tôi** tối nay đi chơi. Sao bạn không đi cùng?
- I have a lot of books, but I haven’t read **any of them**. ‖ Tôi có nhiều sách, nhưng chưa đọc **cuốn nào**.
- ‘How many of these books have you read?’ ‘**None of them**.’ ‖ ‘Bạn đã đọc bao nhiêu cuốn trong số này?’ ‘**Chưa cuốn nào**.’
@rules
A: Noun alone = in general (children, money); the/these/my + noun = specific. ‖ Danh từ đứng một mình = nói chung (children, money); the/these/my + danh từ = cụ thể.
B1: all/most/some/any/no + noun (in general) – no ‘of’ (Most people, not Most of people). ‖ all/most/some/any/no + danh từ (nói chung) – không có ‘of’ (Most people, không nói Most of people).
B2: all (of)/most of/some of/any of/none of + the/this/these/my … ‖ all (of)/most of/some of/any of/none of + the/this/these/my …
C: all/most/some/any/none + of + it/them/us/you. ‖ all/most/some/any/none + of + it/them/us/you.
