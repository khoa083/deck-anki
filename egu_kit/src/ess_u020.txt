I **saw** Paula **yesterday**.
(not I have seen)

(not Where have you been)

Use the past to ask **When** … **?** or **What time** … **?**:
**When did** you **buy** your computer? (not When have you bought)
**What time did** Andy **go** out? (not What time has Andy gone out)
