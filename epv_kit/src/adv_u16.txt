### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- break up sth or break sth up — if an event breaks up a period of time, it makes it more interesting by being different to what you are doing for the rest of the time (units 16)
- bring forward sth or bring sth forward — to change the date or time of an event so that it happens earlier than planned (units 16)
- come around/round — if a regular event comes around, it happens at its usual time (units 16)
- come up — if an event is coming up, it will happen soon (units 16)
- creep up on sb — if a date or an event creeps up on someone, it seems to come or happen sooner than they were expecting (units 16)
- drag out (sth) or drag (sth) out — to continue for longer than is necessary, or to make something do this (units 16)
- eke out sth or eke sth out — to use something slowly or carefully because you only have a small supply of it (units 16)
- fit in — to be able to be done between other activities (units 16)
- free up sth or free sth up — to make time or money available for a particular use by not using it in another way (units 16)
- fritter away sth or fritter sth away — to waste something [esp. money, time] by using it in a careless way for unimportant things (units 16)
- be getting on — mainly British & Australian informal when time is getting on, it is getting late (units 16)
- hold over sth or hold sth over — to delay something and to arrange to do it at a later time (units 16)
- hurry along — to make someone do something more quickly, or to make something happen more quickly (units 16)
- lead up to sth — if a period of time or a series of events leads up to an event or activity, it happens until that event or activity begins (units 16)
- pass sb by — if an event or opportunity passes you by, you do not notice it or you do not get any advantage from it (units 16)
- pull your socks up — to make an effort to improve (units 16)
- put back sth or put sth back — to change the date or time of an event so that it happens later than planned (units 16)
- set aside sth or set sth aside — to use something, especially time, for one purpose and no other purpose (units 16)
- set back sb/sth or set sb/sth back — to make something happen more slowly, or to make something happen later than it should happen (units 16)
- slip away — if a period of time slips away, it seems to pass quickly (units 16)
- space out sth or space sth out — to arrange things so that there is enough space or time between them (units 16, 25)
- spin out sth or spin sth out — to make something continue for as long as possible (units 16)
