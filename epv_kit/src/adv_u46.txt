1	 Draw back means to move away because you are surprised or afraid, and artists ‘draw’ pictures.
2	 Zip along means to move very quickly, and the phrasal verb for fastening a jacket which has a zip 
is ‘zip up’.
3	 Squash up means to move closer together in order to make space for someone else, and ‘squash’ 
can also be a kind of juice drink.
4	 Double back means to turn and go back in the direction you came from; identical twins could be 
called ‘doubles’.
5	 Stole is also the past tense of ‘steal’ meaning to take something without the owner’s permission.

### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- clear out — informal to leave a place (units 46)
- creep up on sb — to move closer to someone, usually from behind, without being seen by them (units 46)
- double back — to turn and go back in the direction that you have come from (units 46)
- draw back — to move away from someone or something, usually because you are surprised or afraid (units 46)
- drop back — if you are moving forward in a group of people and you drop back, you move to a position nearer the back (units 46)
- gain on sb/sth — to get nearer to someone or something that you are chasing (units 46)
- hang back — to not move forwards, usually because you are shy or afraid (units 46)
- pile into swh — to enter a place or vehicle quickly and not in an organised way (units 46)
- pile out (of) — to leave a place or vehicle quickly and not in an organised way (units 46)
- push on — to continue travelling somewhere (units 46)
- squash up — if people who are sitting or standing together squash up, they move closer together in order to make space for someone else (units 46)
- stand back — to move a short distance away from something or someone (units 46)
- steal away — to leave a place quietly without anyone knowing (units 46)
- stumble on sth/sb — to find or meet by chance (units 46)
- trip over (sth) — to fall or almost fall because you have accidentally hit your foot against something while walking or running (units 46)
- walk in on sb — to go into a room and see what someone is doing when they did not want anyone to see them (units 46)
- zip along (sth) — informal to move very quickly (units 46)
