### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- back onto sth — if a building backs onto something, its back faces that thing (units 49)
- blend in/into sth — if something or someone blends in, they look or seem the same as the things or people around them and so you do not notice them (units 49)
- boarded-up — adj covered with pieces of wood (units 49)
- branch off — if a road or path branches off, it goes in another direction (units 2, 49)
- be bursting with sth — to be very full with something (units 49)
- close off sth or close sth off — to put something across the entrance to something, in order to prevent people from entering it (units 49)
- do up sth or do sth up — to repair something, or to improve the appearance of something, especially a building (units 49)
- dot sth with sth — if a place is dotted with something, it has many of them, all over the place (units 49)
- encroach on sth — to gradually cover more and more of an area of land (units 49)
- fall in — if a structure [e.g. roof, ceiling] falls in, it drops to the ground because it is weak or damaged (units 49)
- go up — if a building or sign goes up, it is built or it is fixed into position (units 49)
- lay out sth or lay sth out — to design the way in which a house, city, or garden is built or created (units 49)
- mock-up — n a model showing how something will look when it is built (units 49)
- offset — adj if something is offset by something else it is compensated for (units 49)
- open off sth — if an area opens off another area, you can enter one from the other directly (units 49)
- reduce sth to sth — to destroy something that has been built [e.g. building, city] (units 49)
- run-down — adj shabby, in disrepair (units 49)
- be set back — if a building is set back, it is a little distance from the road (units 49)
- set off sth or set sth off — to make something look attractive, usually by providing a very different colour (units 49)
- oak up sth or soak sth up — to enjoy the effects of an experience (units 49)
- be steeped in sth — to have a lot of (particularly tradition or history) (units 49)
- stretch out — if an area of land stretches out, it continues over a long distance (units 49)
