### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- calm down (sb) or calm (sb) down — to stop feeling upset, angry, or excited, or to make someone stop feeling this way (units 30)
- Come off it! — something that you say in order to tell someone that you do not believe them or that you disagree with them or are angry with them (units 30)
- Come on! — something that you say in order to tell someone that you do not believe them or that you disagree with them or are angry with them (units 30)
- Dream on! — something that you say in order to tell someone that what they are hoping for is not possible and will not happen (units 30)
- drink up (sth) or drink (sth) up — to completely finish your drink (units 30)
- Eat up! — something that you say to someone, especially a child, in order to tell them to eat their food (units 30)
- Get off (sb/sth)! — something that you say in order to tell someone to stop touching someone or something (units 30)
- go ahead — slightly informal something that you say to someone to give them permission to start to do something (units 30)
- go easy on sb — to treat someone in a gentle way, especially when you want to be or should be more severe (units 30)
- Go for it! — something that you say to encourage someone to try and achieve something (units 30)
- Go on! — something that you say to tell someone that you do not believe what they have just told you (units 30)
- Go on then! — something that you say to encourage someone to do something (units 30)
- Grow up! — informal something that you say to an adult in order to tell them to stop behaving stupidly (units 30)
- Hang about/on! — something that you say to tell someone to stop doing or saying something (units 30)
- Hold on! — to tell someone to stop doing something for a very short while (units 30)
- Hurry up! — informal to tell someone to move more quickly or do a task more quickly (units 30)
- keep it up — to continue to do something, especially to work hard or to do good work (units 30, 59)
- Roll on sth! — British informal something that you say in order to show that you are looking forward to a time or event (units 30)
- shut (sb) up — to stop talking or making a noise, or to make someone do this (units 30)
- Shut up! — to tell someone to stop talking (units 30)
- Take it away! — something that you say in musical contexts to tell someone to start playing or singing (units 30, 60)
- Wake up! — informal something you say to tell someone to listen to what you are saying when they have not been listening (units 30)
- Watch out! — something you say to tell someone to be careful so that they can avoid danger or an accident (units 30)
