### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- keep (sb) ahead — to continue to be more advanced and successful than other people, or to make sure that someone is more advanced or successful than other people (units 59)
- keep at sth — to continue to do something (until it is finished) (units 59)
- keep back sth or keep sth back — to not tell someone everything you know about a situation or an event that has happened (units 59)
- keep down sth or keep sth down — if you keep the noise of something [e.g. music, voice] down, you stop it from becoming too loud (units 21, 59)
- keep sb/sth from doing sth — to prevent someone from doing something, especially work, by spending time with them (units 59)
- keep sb in — to make a child stay at school or at home, especially as a punishment (units 59)
- keep in with sb — British & Australian to continue to be friendly with someone, especially because they can help you (units 5, 59)
- keep (sb) off sth — to not eat, drink or use something that can harm you (units 59)
- keep on doing sth — to continue to do something, or to do something again and again (units 59)
- keep out of sth — to not become involved in something (units 59)
- keep to sth — if you keep to the point, you do not write or talk about other topics (units 59)
- keep sth to yourself — to keep something secret (units 59)
- keep up — to be able to understand or deal with something that is happening or changing very fast (units 24, 59)
- keep it up — to continue to do something, especially to work hard or to do good work (units 30, 59)
- keep sb up — to make someone go to bed later than they usually do (units 59)
- keep up sth or keep sth up — to not allow something that is at a high level to fall to a lower level (units 59)
- upkeep — n keeping a building in good condition, usually by providing money to repair it (units 59)
