replace around,

Particular purpose or without concentrating.

- bash sth/sb about to treat something or someone in a rough way (18)
- fool around to spend time having fun or behaving ina silly way (18)
- hang about/around/round (swh) informal to spend time somewhere, usually without doing verymuch (18)
- laze about/around to relax and enjoy yourself by doing very little (18)
- lie about/around informal to spend time lying down and doing very little (18)
- look around/round (swh) to walk through a building or around a place and look at the things in it (18)
- mess about/around to spend time playing or doing things with no particular purpose (18)
- mess about/around to behave stupidly or to waste time doing unimportant things (18, 69)
- mess sb about/around informal to treat someone badly, especially by changing your mind a lot or not doing what you have promised (1, 18)
- mill about/around (swh) to walk around a particular place or area, usually while waiting for Something (18)
- order sb about/around to tell someone what they should do all the time (18, 31)
- see sb about/around to see someone in the places you usually seethem (18)
- show sb around/round (swh) to go with someone to a place that they have not visited before and show them the interesting parts (18)
- sit about/around (swh) to spend time sitting down and doing very little (18)
- stand about/around/round to spend time standing in a place waiting for someone or doing very little (18)
- stick around informal to stay somewhere for a period of time (18)
- take around/round sb or take sb around/round (swh) to walk through a building or to visit a place with someone, showing them the most interesting or important parts (18)
- wait about/around to stay in one place without doing anything while you are waiting for something to happen (18)
