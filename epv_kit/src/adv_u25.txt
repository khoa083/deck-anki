### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- do up sth or do sth up — to wrap something [esp. present] in paper (units 25)
- double up as sth — if something designed for one purpose can double up as something else, it can also be used for something else (units 25)
- fix up sth or fix sth up — to arrange something [esp. meeting] (units 25)
- fold up (sth) or fold (sth) up — to make something [e.g. cloth, paper, chair] into a smaller, neater and usually flatter shape by folding it, or to be able to be folded in this way (units 25)
- gather up sth or gather sth up — to collect several things, especially from different places, and put them together (units 25)
- gear sth to/towards sth/sb — to design something so that it is suitable for a particular purpose, situation, or group of people (units 25)
- hoard away sth or hoard sth away — to put a supply of something in a safe place so that you can use it in the future (units 25)
- know sth from sth — to know the difference between something and something else, so that you can recognise either of them (units 25)
- line up sth or line sth up — to move something in order to make it straight or level with something else (units 25)
- pile up (sth) or pile (sth) up — to become a pile, or to make a lot of things into a pile by putting them on top of each other (units 25)
- prop up sth or prop sth up — to make something stay in a particular position by putting something underneath or against it (units 25)
- rank (sth) among sth — to have a particular position in a list that has been arranged in order of quality (units 25)
- round up sb/sth or round sb/sth up — to find and gather together a group of people or animals (units 25)
- see about sth — to deal with something, or to arrange for something to be done (units 25)
- separate off sth or separate sth off — to remove something from a large group of things (units 25)
- separate out sb/sth or separate sb/sth — out to divide a group of people or things into smaller groups (units 25)
- sift through sth — to examine a large collection of something, especially papers, usually in order to discover something or to decide what is important (units 25)
- space out sth or space sth out — to arrange things so that there is enough space or time between them (units 16, 25)
- stack up sth or stack sth up — to arrange things in a tall pile (units 25)
