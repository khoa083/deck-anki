Tip
Note how the meaning of a phrasal verb can sometimes vary depending on whether it is used with 
an object or not. For example, come across (see above), when it is used with an object means ‘find 
or meet something/somebody by chance’: I came across an old photo of us when we were at 
university the other night. Make a note of any verbs whose meanings change in this way.

### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- bore into sb — if someone’s eyes bore into you, they look at you very hard and make you feel nervous (units 40)
- bring out sth or bring sth out — to make a particular quality more noticeable (units 40)
- come across — to behave in a way which makes people believe that you have a particular characteristic (units 40, 56)
- descend to sth/doing sth — to behave badly in a way that other people would not expect you to (units 40)
- draw out sb or draw sb out — to help someone who is shy to feel more confident (units 40)
- be getting on — informal if someone is getting on, they are old (units 40)
- grow out of sth — if a child grows out of an interest, way of behaving, or illness, they stop having or doing it because they have become older (units 40)
- have sb down as sth — to think that someone is a particular type of person, especially when they are not in fact like that (units 40)
- light up (sth) or light (sth) up — if your face or eyes light up, or if something [e.g. smile] lights them up, you suddenly look very happy or excited (units 40)
- outspoken — adj if someone is outspoken, they express their opinions even though other people might be offended by them (units 4, 40)
- pride yourself on sth/doing sth — (always reflexive) to be proud of a quality you have or of something you do (units 27, 40)
- screw up sb or screw sb up — informal to make someone feel confused or unhappy about themselves and their life (units 40)
- shine through (sth) — if a quality that someone has shines through or shines through something, that quality is very easily noticed (units 40)
- toughen up — to become stronger and more able to deal with problems, or to make someone become this way (units 40)
- vouch for sb — to say that you know someone and that you can promise that they have a good character or good skills (units 40)
