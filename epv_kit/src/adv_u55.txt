### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- (not) agree with sb — if a type of food or drink does not agree with you, it makes you feel slightly ill (units 55)
- cut out sth or cut sth out — to stop eating or drinking something, usually in order to improve your health (units 55)
- dip sth in (sth) — to quickly put something into something else and then take it out again, especially biscuits in tea or coffee (units 55)
- disagree with sb — if a type of food disagrees with you, it makes you feel slightly ill or uncomfortable (units 55)
- drink to sb/sth — to hold up your glass before drinking from it in order to celebrate something or to wish someone success or happiness (units 55)
- eat in — to have a meal at home, not in a restaurant (units 55)
- fill up sb or fill sb up — if food fills someone up, it makes them feel as if they have eaten enough (units 55)
- fry-up — n a quick meal made of fried food (units 55)
- gobble down/up sth or gobble sth down/ up — informal to eat very quickly (units 55)
- help sb to sth — to put food onto a plate for yourself (units 55)
- pick at sth — to eat only a small amount of a meal because you are not hungry or because you are feeling ill (units 55)
- put on sth or put sth on — if a person or animal puts on weight, they become heavier (units 26, 55)
- send out for sth — to telephone a restaurant and ask for food to be delivered to your home (units 55)
- serve up sth or serve sth up — to put food on plates and in dishes for people to eat (units 55)
- slice up sth or slice sth up — to cut or divide something into parts (units 55)
- soak up sth or soak sth up — if a dry substance soaks up a liquid, it absorbs it (units 55)
- spill over (sth) — if the liquid in a container spills over, it flows over the edge of the container (units 55)
- wash down sth or wash sth down — to drink something while you are eating food or taking medicine in order to help you swallow it (units 55)
- water down sth or water sth down — to add water to a drink, especially an alcoholic drink (units 10, 55)
- water down sth or water sth down — to make an idea or opinion less strong in order to make more people agree with it, or to make a plan or suggestion more acceptable (units 10, 55)
- wolf down sth or wolf sth down — to eat something very quickly because you are very hungry (units 55)
