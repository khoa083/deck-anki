### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- break off sth or break sth off — to separate a part from a larger piece (units 47)
- bring up sb or bring sb up — to look after a child and educate them until they are old enough to look after themselves (units 47)
- chase sb/sth off/out or chase off/out sb/sth — to run after a person or an animal in a threatening way in order to make them leave (units 47)
- come in — if the tide (= the regular change in the level of the sea) comes in, the sea comes higher up the beach (units 47, 56)
- come out — if the sun, the moon, or a star comes out, it appears in the sky; also applies to flowers and leaves which appear on plants in spring (units 47)
- cut down sth or cut sth down — if you cut down a tree or bush, you make it fall to the ground by cutting it near the bottom (units 47)
- die out — to become more and more rare and then disappear completely (units 47)
- dig up sth or dig sth up — to take something out of the ground by digging (units 47)
- dry out (sth) or dry (sth) out — to make something dry, or to become dry (units 47)
- dry up — if an area of water [esp. river, lake] dries up, the water in it disappears (units 47)
- eat away at sth — to gradually destroy something by continuously damaging it or taking little parts of it away (units 47)
- feed on sth — if an animal feeds on a particular type of food, it usually eats that food (units 47)
- fend for yourself — to take care of yourself without needing help from other people (units 47)
- flush out sb/sth or flush sb/sth out — to force a person or animal to come out of the place they are hiding in (units 47)
- freeze over — if water freezes over, it becomes covered with ice (units 2, 47)
- go in — if the sun or moon goes in, it becomes hidden behind a cloud (units 47)
- go out — if the sea or the tide (= movement of the sea in and out) goes out, it moves away from the beach (units 47)
- move in — to begin living in a new house or area (units 47)
- offshoot — n plant which has developed from a larger plant (units 47)
- overcast — adj if the sky is overcast, it is grey and covered in cloud (units 47)
- pick up — to lift something by using a bill (birds) (units 47)
- prey on sth — to catch for food (units 47)
- pull down sth or pull sth down — to destroy a structure because it is not wanted anymore (units 47)
- pull up sth or pull sth up — to remove something from the ground (units 47)
- send out sth or send sth out — if a plant sends out something [e.g. roots, shoots], it grows (units 47)
- take over sth or take sth over — to get control of an area of land or a political organisation, usually by using force (units 47)
- wash away sth or wash sth away — if water [e.g. rain, flood] washes something away, it carries it away (units 47)
- watch over sb/sth — to protect or take care of a person or animal (units 47)
