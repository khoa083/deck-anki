Included in the Mini dictionary are:

break away break down break in(to) break off break out break up

While they each have different meanings, break seems to suggest a sudden, sometimes violent or
negative event which changes a situation; for example, a marriage breaks up, a disease/epidemic
breaks out, a group of people break away from another group.
