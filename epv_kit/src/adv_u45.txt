### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- blurt out sth or blurt sth out — to say something suddenly and without thinking, especially because you are excited or nervous (units 45)
- bombard sb with sth — to direct a lot of something [e.g. questions, letters] at one person (units 45)
- clam up — informal to become silent or to refuse to speak about something, usually because you are shy or afraid (units 45)
- cut in (on) (sth) — to interrupt what someone is saying by saying something yourself (units 11, 45)
- drag sth out of sb — to make someone tell you something that they do not want to tell you (units 45)
- dry up — to stop speaking when you are acting or making a speech, especially because you suddenly forget what to say next (units 45)
- engage sb in conversation — if you engage someone in conversation, you try to start a conversation with them (units 45)
- expand on sth — to give more details about something you have said or written (units 45)
- explain away sth or explain sth away — to give a reason for something bad happening which makes other people think that it is not so bad or that it is not your fault (units 45)
- gloss over sth — to avoid discussing something, or to discuss something without talking about the details in order to make it seem unimportant (units 6, 45)
- go on at sb — to criticise someone continuously (units 45)
- hold forth — to talk about a particular subject for a long time, often in a way that other people find boring (units 45)
- lead into sth — if a subject you are talking about or a discussion leads into another subject or discussion, it is the reason why you start talking about the second subject or start the second discussion (units 45)
- put sth to sb — to ask someone a question, or to state an opinion to someone which they are likely to disagree with (units 45)
- reel off sth or reel sth off — to say a long list of things quickly and without stopping (units 45)
- run through sth — to explain or read something to someone quickly (units 45)
- sound out sb/sth or sound sb/sth out — to talk to someone in order to discover what they think about an idea or plan (units 45, 51)
- talk at sb — to talk to someone without listening to them or allowing them to speak (units 45)
- witter on — British informal to talk for a long time about unimportant things (units 45)
- wring sth out of sb — to force or persuade someone to give you money or information (units 45)
