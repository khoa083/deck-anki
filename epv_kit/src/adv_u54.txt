Computers
Several verbs related to computers are based on the verb go, meaning access or use something. 
You can go on a computer, the Internet, a website.
You can go to a menu, an item on a menu, a particular web page or part of a page, or a link.

### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- back-up — n support, help or something that you have arranged in case your main plans or equipment go wrong (units 3, 54)
- call up sth or call sth up — to find and show information on a computer screen (units 54)
- come on — if something [e.g. lights, heating] or a supply of something [e.g. water, electricity] comes on, it starts working (units 54)
- come up — if information comes up on a computer screen, it appears there (units 54)
- drop-down menu — a list of choices which appears on a computer screen (units 54)
- fade in — if the sound or picture of a film or recording fades in, or if you fade it in, it becomes gradually louder or brighter so that you can hear it or see it clearly (units 54)
- fade out — if the sound or picture of a film or recording fades out, or if you fade it out, it gradually becomes quieter or less clear until you cannot hear or see it any more (units 54)
- go off — if a light or machine goes off, it stops working (units 54)
- go on sth — to use a computer or the Internet, or to visit a website (units 54)
- go to (swh) — to click on a menu, a menu item, a particular web page or part of a page, or a link when using a computer (units 54)
- pick up sth or pick sth up — if a device picks up a signal or programmes broadcast by a radio station, it receives them (units 1, 54)
- plug into sth — if a piece of electrical equipment plugs into a supply of electricity or another piece of electrical equipment, it works by being connected to that supply of electricity or that piece of equipment (units 54)
- pop-up adverts — n advertisements that open quickly on a computer screen in front of what you are working on (units 54)
- print off sth or print sth off — to print a particular number of copies of something (units 6, 54)
- seize up — if part of your body or a machine seizes up, it stops moving or working in the normal way (units 54)
- set up (sth) or set (sth) up — to get all the necessary equipment ready for a particular activity (units 54)
- tune into sth — to turn on the radio or television in order to listen to or watch a particular programme (units 54)
- type in — to write something using a computer (units 54)
- warm up (sth) or warm (sth) up — if an engine or machine warms up, or if you warm it up, it starts working so that it becomes warm enough to work well (units 54)
- wipe off sth or wipe sth off — to remove information stored on part of a computer [esp. memory, hard drive] (units 54)
- wire up sth/sb or wire sth/sb up — to connect something or someone to a piece of electrical equipment by using electrical wires (units 54)
