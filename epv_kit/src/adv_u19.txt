1	 Wind up means to finish/terminate something. It is also what you do to a non-electric clock or 
watch to make it work.
2	 Sprout up means to suddenly appear. A Brussels sprout is also a vegetable.
3	 Press on means to continue in a determined way. Dry cleaners also press clothes (remove creases 
by using an iron or a pressing machine).
4	 Spring up means to appear suddenly. A trampoline has springs, which contract and expand, 
making it possible for a person to bounce up and down on it.
5	 Training shoes are worn by athletes when running. To run out of money means to have no  
money left.

### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- bottom out — if a situation, level, or rate that is getting worse bottoms out, it reaches the lowest point and remains at that level or amount, usually before improving (units 19)
- break up (sth) or break (sth) up — if an occasion when people meet [e.g. meeting, party] breaks up, or if someone breaks it up, it ends and people start to leave (units 19)
- dry up — if a supply of something dries up, it ends (units 19)
- fall apart — if an organisation, system, or agreement falls apart, it fails or stops working effectively (units 19)
- finish with sth — to stop using or needing something (units 19)
- get up to sth — to reach a particular place in something that you are doing and to stop there (units 19)
- hang on to/onto sth/sb — to keep someone or something (units 19)
- magic away sth or magic sth away — to make something disappear so quickly that it seems as if you have used magic (units 19)
- open up (sth) or open (sth) up — to create a new opportunity or possibility (units 15, 19)
- peter out — if an energetic activity or a strong emotion peters out, it gradually becomes less energetic or strong until it stops completely (units 19)
- press on — to continue doing something in a determined way (units 13, 19)
- run out — if a supply of something runs out, there is none left because it has all been used (units 19)
- spring up — if something springs up, it suddenly appears or begins to exist (units 19)
- sprout up — if a large number of things sprout up, they suddenly appear or begin to exist (units 19)
- whittle away sth or whittle sth away or whittle away at sth — to gradually reduce the size or importance of something until it does not exist any more (units 19)
- wind up (sth) or wind (sth) up — to finish an activity (units 19)
