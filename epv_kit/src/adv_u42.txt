### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- call in (on) — British & Australian to visit a place or person for a short time, usually while you are going somewhere else (units 6, 42)
- deal with sth — to take action in order to achieve something, or in order to solve a problem (units 42)
- drop by — British & Australian informal to make a short visit to someone in their home, usually without arranging it before (units 42)
- get into sth — to become interested in an activity or subject, or to start being involved in an activity (units 42, 57)
- get-together — n an informal meeting or party (units 42)
- get up to sth — to do something, especially something that other people think is wrong (units 42, 57)
- go on to swh — to go to a particular place after going somewhere else (units 42)
- hang out — informal to spend a lot of time in a particular place, or to spend a lot of time with someone (units 42)
- hook up — US informal to meet someone for a particular purpose (units 42)
- letdown — n something which is not as good as you thought it would be (units 3, 42)
- move away — to leave the place or area where you live and go and live in another place (units 42)
- move on — to move forward in one’s life and not look back to the past (units 42)
- pass by (swh) — British to visit somewhere for a short time, usually while you are going somewhere else (units 42)
- roll up — to arrive at a particular place or event, usually late (units 42)
- shoot off — British & Australian informal to leave somewhere very quickly (units 42)
- sleep off sth or sleep sth off — to sleep until you feel better, especially after too much alcohol (units 42)
- take out sb or take sb out — to go somewhere and do something with someone, usually something that you have planned or paid for (units 42, 60)
- treat sb to sth — to buy or pay for something for someone else (units 42)
- turn away sb or turn sb away — to refuse to allow someone to enter a place, usually because there is no more space (units 42)
