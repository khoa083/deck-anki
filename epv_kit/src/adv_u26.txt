### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- add up — to increase and become a large number or amount (units 26)
- amount to sth — to become a particular amount (units 26)
- average out at sth — to have as the average number (units 26)
- balance out — to make things equal (units 26)
- build up (sth) or build (sth) up — to increase in amount, size, or strength, or to make something increase in amount, size, or strength (units 3, 26)
- bump up sth or bump sth up — informal to increase the size or amount of something [e.g. price] by a large amount (units 26)
- fall off — if the amount, rate, or quality of something falls off, it becomes smaller or lower (units 26)
- flatten out — if the rate of something flattens out, or if something flattens it out, it stops increasing and decreasing and begins to stay at the same level (units 26)
- knock down sb/sth or knock sb/sth down — informal to reduce a price, or to persuade someone to reduce the price of something that they are selling (units 10, 26)
- level off/out — if a rate or amount levels off, it stops rising or falling and it stays at the same level (units 26)
- push up sth or push sth up — to increase the amount, number, or value of something (units 26)
- put on sth or put sth on — if a person or animal puts on weight, they become heavier (units 26, 55)
- round down sth or round sth down — to decrease a number to the nearest whole amount (units 26)
- round up sth or round sth up — to increase a number to the nearest whole amount (units 26)
- slim down (sth) or slim (sth) down — to become smaller in size, often by employing fewer people, or to make something smaller (units 26)
- tail off — to decrease in amount or level (units 26)
- thin out — if a large number of people or things thin out, they become fewer in number (units 26)
