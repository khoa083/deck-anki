### LEFT PAGE (lý thuyết):
Some phrasal verbs with on share a meaning of dependence/reliance, e.g. depend on*, rely 
on*, count on*, hinge on*, hang on*, ride on.
On can also convey the idea of continuing in an irritating or boring way, for example, harp on 
[talk repeatedly about something in a way that others find boring or irritating], keep on, go 
on (and on), ramble on [talk or write for a very long time in a boring way] and drone on [talk 
for a long time in a very boring way and in a monotonous voice].
Don’t keep on about the garden! I’ll mow the lawn tomorrow.
We had a tedious lecture from the boss. She just went on and on about punctuality.
He droned on for hours about ancient civilisations; everyone fell asleep.
Read on, press on, live on and dwell on* [think or talk about a particular subject for too long] 
also emphasise continuation over time.
Grouping verbs in this way can help you to remember them.

### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- count on sth — to expect something to happen, and make plans which depend on this thing happening (units 13)
- depend on sth/sb — to need the help or support of something or someone in order to survive or continue as before (units 13)
- drone on — to talk for a long time in a very boring way (units 13, 21)
- dwell on sth — to think or talk about a particular subject for too long (units 13)
- focus (sth) on sth — to give a lot of attention to one particular activity, situation or idea (units 13)
- frown on sth — to believe that something is wrong and that you should not do it (units 13)
- go on — to talk in an annoying way about something for a long time (units 13)
- hang on sth — to depend on something (units 13)
- happen on sb/sth — to find something or meet someone without planning to (units 13)
- harp on — to talk continually about something in a way that other people find boring or annoying (units 13)
- hinge on sth — to depend on something or be very influenced by it (units 13)
- keep on — slightly informal to talk in an annoying way about something for a long time (units 13)
- lead on sb or lead sb on — to make someone do something bad by encouraging them or annoying them until they do it (units 13)
- live on — to continue to exist (units 13)
- press on — to continue doing something in a determined way (units 13, 19)
- ramble on — to talk or write for a long time about things that are not interesting to other people (units 13)
- read on — to continue reading and read the next part of something (units 13)
- rely on sth/sb — to need something or someone in order to survive, be successful, or work correctly (units 13)
- ride on sth — if something important [e.g. reputation, money] rides on something else, it depends on it (units 13)
- spring sth on sb — to tell someone some news that surprises them (units 13)
