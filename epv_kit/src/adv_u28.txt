### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- bear up — to deal with a very sad or difficult situation in a brave and determined way (units 28)
- botch up sth or botch sth up — informal to spoil a piece of work by doing it badly (units 28)
- break down — if a discussion, system or relationship breaks down, it fails because of a disagreement or problem (units 28)
- be caught up in sth — to become involved in an activity or situation which prevents you from moving or making progress (units 28)
- clear up sth or clear sth up — to give or find an explanation for something, or to deal with a problem or disagreement (units 28)
- fall back on sth — to use something when other things have failed, or when there are no other choices (units 28)
- grasp at sth — to quickly use or accept an opportunity to do or have something, especially because you are unhappy with the present situation (units 28)
- hang over sb/sth — if a problem or threat hangs over a person or situation, it exists and makes people worry about what is going to happen (units 28)
- iron out sth or iron sth out — to find a way of solving small difficulties or problems, or to find a way of ending a disagreement (units 28)
- land (sb) in sth — to be in a difficult situation, or to cause someone to be in a difficult situation or an unpleasant place [e.g. prison] (units 28)
- live with sth — to accept a difficult or unpleasant situation and continue with your life while it exists (units 28)
- make up for sth — if someone makes up for something bad that they have done, they do something good so that the bad thing does not cause a problem any more (units 28)
- resign yourself to sth — to accept that something we do not want to happen will happen (units 28)
- resort to sth/doing sth — to do something bad in order to achieve what you want, often because it is the only thing you can do to achieve it (units 28)
- be riddled with sth — be full of something, especially something bad or unpleasant (units 5, 28)
- run into sth — if you run into difficulties or problems, you begin to experience them (units 28)
- safeguard against sth — to do things that you hope will stop something unpleasant happening (units 28)
- smooth over sth or smooth sth over — to make a disagreement or problem seem less serious or more easy to deal with, especially by talking to the people involved in it (units 28)
- spill over — if a bad situation or problem spills over, it begins to have an unpleasant effect on another situation or group of people (units 28)
- walk away — to stop being involved in a situation that is difficult to deal with or that does not give you any advantages (units 28)
