### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- bowl over sb or bowl sb over — to surprise or please someone a lot (units 41)
- brim with sth — to have or show a lot of a good emotion or good quality (units 41)
- not care for sth/sb — formal to not like something or someone (units 41)
- choke back/down sth or choke sth back/ down — to force yourself not to show your feelings (units 41)
- come over sb — if a feeling comes over you, you suddenly experience it (units 41)
- feel up to sth/doing sth — to feel physically and mentally strong enough to do something (units 41)
- fight back sth or fight sth back — to try hard not to show an emotion, or to try hard not to let your emotions control what you do (units 41)
- give in to sth — if you give in to an emotion or desire, you stop trying not to feel it and you allow your actions to be controlled by that emotion or desire (units 41)
- go off sb/sth — to stop liking someone or something (units 5, 41)
- go out to sb — if your thoughts or sympathies go out to someone, you feel very sorry for them when they are in a difficult situation (units 41)
- hanker after/for sth — to want something very much, especially something you know you should not want (units 5, 41)
- have sth against sb/sth — to dislike or disagree with someone or something for a particular reason (units 41)
- loosen up — to relax mentally (units 41)
- perk up (sb) or perk (sb) up — to suddenly become happier or more energetic, or to make someone feel this way (units 41)
- run away with sb — if something [e.g. emotions, imagination, enthusiasm] runs away with someone, it makes them do or think stupid things (units 41)
- shake up sb or shake sb up — if an unpleasant experience shakes someone up, it makes them feel shocked and upset (units 41)
- snap out of sth — informal to force yourself to stop feeling sad and upset (units 41)
- spill out (sth) or spill (sth) out — if you spill out an emotion or if emotion spills out, you express it, usually by talking in an uncontrolled way (units 41)
- summon up sth or summon sth up — to try hard to find a particular quality [e.g. courage, energy] in yourself because you need it in order to do something (units 41)
- turn (sb) against sth/sb — to decide not to like or agree with someone or something, or to make someone do this (units 41)
- work yourself into sth — to make yourself become very angry or upset (units 41)
