### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- come down — if you come down when you are suggesting a price for something, you suggest or agree to a lower price (units 10)
- come down — if a story or tradition comes down, it is passed from older people to younger people so that it continues (units 10, 56)
- get sb down — to make someone feel unhappy (units 10, 57)
- go down — if a computer system goes down, it stops working (units 10, 58)
- go down — to be remembered as part of something (units 10)
- go down — well-received (units 10)
- gun down sb or gun sb down — to shoot someone and kill or seriously injure them, often when they cannot defend themselves (units 10)
- knock down sb/sth or knock sb/sth down — informal to reduce a price, or to persuade someone to reduce the price of something that they are selling (units 10, 26)
- pin down sb or pin sb down — to force someone to stay in a horizontal position by holding them (units 10)
- put down sb or put sb down — to put someone’s name on a list or document, usually in order to arrange for them to do something (units 10)
- put down sth or put sth down — to kill an animal because it is very old or very ill (units 10)
- shout down sb or shout sb down — to shout in order to prevent someone who is saying something that you disagree with from being heard (units 10)
- shut down (sth) or shut (sth) — down if a machine shuts down or someone shuts it down, it stops operating (units 10)
- slam down sth or slam sth — down to put something down with a lot of force (units 10)
- take down sth or take sth down — to write something, especially something that someone says (units 10)
- tie down sth/sb or tie sth/sb down — to fasten something or someone in a particular position, especially by using ropes (units 10)
- tie sb down — to stop someone from being free to do what they want to do (units 10, 29)
- turn down sth or turn sth down — to reduce the amount of sound or heat that is produced by a device [e.g. television, radio, oven] (units 10)
- water down sth or water sth down — to add water to a drink, especially an alcoholic drink (units 10, 55)
- water down sth or water sth down — to make an idea or opinion less strong in order to make more people agree with it, or to make a plan or suggestion more acceptable (units 10, 55)
