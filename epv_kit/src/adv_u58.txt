### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- go after sb — to chase or follow someone in order to catch them (units 58)
- go after / for sth — to try to get something that you want [e.g. job] (units 58)
- go ahead — if an event goes ahead, it happens (units 58)
- go around doing sth — if someone goes around doing something, they spend their time behaving badly or doing something that is unpleasant for other people (units 58)
- go back over sth — to examine or think about something again or after it has happened (units 58)
- go by sth — to use information or experience that you have of someone or something in order to help you decide what to do or what to think about that person or thing (units 58)
- go down — if a computer system goes down, it stops working (units 10, 58)
- go down — to become worse in quality (units 58)
- go down — if part of your body that is bigger than usual because of an illness or injury goes down, it starts to return to its usual size (units 43, 58)
- go for sb — to attack someone (units 58)
- go for sth — if something goes for a particular amount of money, it is sold for that amount (units 58)
- go forward — if someone’s name goes forward, it is suggested that that person should compete for an elected position or a job (units 58)
- go into sth — to start an activity, or start to be in a particular state or condition (units 58)
- go off — to happen in a particular way (units 58)
- go over sth — to think about something that has happened or something that was said (units 33, 58)
- go over to sth — to leave one group or organisation and join a group or organisation that is competing against them (units 6, 58)
- go through sth — to experience an unpleasant or difficult situation or event (units 58)
