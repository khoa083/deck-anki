### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- beaver away — informal to work hard at something for a long time, especially something you are writing (units 31)
- be bogged down — to become so involved in the details of something that you cannot achieve anything (units 31)
- call out sb or call sb out — to order workers to strike (= to refuse to work because of an argument with an employer) (units 31)
- coast along — to do only the things that you have to do without trying to go faster or be more successful (units 31)
- cobble together sth or cobble sth together — to make something quickly and not very carefully (units 31)
- be cut out for sth — to have the right qualities for something, especially a job (units 31)
- ease off/up — to start to work less or to do things with less energy (units 31)
- farm out sth or farm sth out — to give work to other people instead of doing it yourself (units 31)
- fling yourself into — to start to spend a lot of your time and energy doing something (units 31)
- get off — to leave the place where you work, usually at the end of the day (units 31, 57)
- go in to sth — to choose a particular type of work as your job (units 31)
- hand in sth or hand sth in — to tell your boss officially that you do not want to do your job anymore (units 31)
- knuckle down — to start to work or study hard, especially if you have not been working very hard before (units 31)
- pack in sth or pack sth in — informal to stop doing something, especially a job (units 31)
- pass over sb or pass sb over — to not give someone a job or a higher position and give it to someone else who is younger or less experienced (units 31)
- plug away — informal to work hard at something for a long time (units 31)
- run around/round after sb — informal to do a lot of things for someone else, especially when they should be able to do more for themselves (units 9, 31)
- see sth through — to continue doing a job or activity until it is finished, especially when it is difficult (units 31)
- slog away — informal to keep working very hard, usually for a long time (units 31)
- step aside — to leave a job or position, especially so that someone else can do it (units 31)
