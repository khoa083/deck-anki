### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- capitalise on sth — to use a situation in order to achieve something good for yourself (units 27)
- carry off sth or carry sth off — to succeed in doing or achieving something difficult (units 27)
- come out — if you describe how something or someone comes out at the end of a process or activity, you describe what condition they are in or what they have achieved (units 27)
- doom (sb/sth) to sth to — make someone or something sure to fail or suffer in a particular way (units 27)
- fall behind (sb) — to fail to score as many points as another team or player in a competition (units 27)
- fall through — if a plan or agreement falls through, it fails to happen (units 6, 27)
- get by — to have or know just enough of something to be able to deal with a particular situation but not have or know as much as you would like (units 1, 27)
- get (sb) through sth — to succeed in an examination or competition, or to help someone or something do this (units 27, 32)
- lose out to — to be less successful than (units 27)
- pack in sth or pack sth in — informal to manage to do a lot of activities in a period of time (units 27)
- pride yourself on sth/doing sth — (always reflexive) to be proud of a quality you have or of something you do (units 27, 40)
- pull (sb) through (sth) — to succeed in dealing with a difficult period of your life, or to help someone else to do this (units 27)
- rise above sth — to not allow something bad that is happening or being done to you to upset you or to affect your behaviour (units 27)
- sail through (sth) — to succeed very easily, especially in a test, examination etc. (units 5, 27, 32)
- succeed in sth/doing sth — to achieve something that you have been trying to get or do (units 27)
- win back sb/sth or win sb/sth back — to persuade customers to return to using your company rather than competing companies because your company has improved (units 27)
- win out — if a particular emotion or type of behaviour wins out, it is stronger than other emotions or types of behaviour (units 27)
- win through — to finally succeed after trying hard to achieve something (units 27)
