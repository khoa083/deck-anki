### KEY (đáp án bài tập của unit này):
Unit 52
Possible answers:
1	 Its aim was to find out whether claims of fraud were correct or not – and it discovered that the 
claims were justified.
2	 By hushing up a scandal, the company is trying to keep it secret – which suggests that it is a 
scandal which might harm the reputation of the company in some way.
3	 It is implying that the government is not being entirely honest with the public about possible 
health risks.
4	 Because he/she does not want knowledge of the internal disagreements to become public in case 
the government loses public support. In other words, he/she is trying to hide this information in 
the same way in which wallpaper is sometimes used to paper over cracks in a wall.
5	 It sounds as if it condemns their behaviour – this is suggested by ‘cover up’.
6	 The spy has given information about the way in which secret agents spied on diplomats.

### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- bear out sth/sb or bear sth/sb out — to prove that something that someone has said or written [e.g. claim, theory] is true, or to say that someone is telling the truth (units 52)
- catch out sb or catch sb out — to discover that someone is lying or doing something wrong (units 52)
- confide in sb — to tell someone things that you keep secret from other people (units 51, 52)
- cover up (sth) or cover (sth) up — to stop people from discovering the truth about something bad (units 52)
- dig up sth or dig sth up — to discover new facts about a person or situation after a lot of searching (units 52)
- give away sth or give sth away — to let someone know something that should be kept secret, often by mistake (units 52)
- give-away — n something that makes you aware of a fact that someone else was trying to keep secret (units 52)
- hush up sth or hush sth up — to stop the public from finding out about something bad that has happened (units 52)
- let out sth or let sth out — to tell someone about something which was supposed to be a secret (units 52)
- level with sb — informal to tell the truth about something (units 52)
- make out sth — to claim falsely that something is true; to pretend (units 52)
- paper over sth — to hide a disagreement or difficulty and try to make people believe that there is no problem (units 52)
- play along — to pretend to agree with someone, or to do what someone wants for a short time, in order to get something from them or to avoid making them angry (units 52)
- spy on sb/sth — to watch secretly in order to discover information about them (units 52)
