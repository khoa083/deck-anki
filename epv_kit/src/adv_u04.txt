### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- break down — if a machine or vehicle breaks down, it stops working (units 4)
- broken down — adj if a machine or vehicle is broken-down it no longer works (units 4)
- built-in — adj if something is built-in, it is made so that it fits into part of a wall or room (units 4)
- downcast — adj sad and depressed (units 4)
- fallback — adj a fallback position is something you use when other things have failed, or when there are no other choices (units 4)
- foldaway — adj a foldaway bed has parts that can be folded so that it is smaller and can be stored somewhere (units 4)
- fold-up — adj a fold-up chair can be made into a smaller, neater and flatter shape by folding it (units 4)
- forthcoming — adj a forthcoming event, action, or product is one which will happen or become available soon (units 4)
- go-ahead — adj using new methods and ideas to succeed (units 4)
- go on — to continue to exist or happen (units 4)
- left out — adj if someone feels left out, they are unhappy because they have not been included in an activity or conversation (units 4)
- live-in — adj a live-in nanny lives at the home of the children she cares for (units 4)
- off-putting — adj slightly unpleasant or worrying so that you are discouraged from getting involved in any way (units 4)
- ongoing — adj if a problem is ongoing, it continues to be a problem (units 4)
- outdated — adj old-fashioned and therefore not as good or as fashionable as something that is modern (units 4)
- outgoing — adj if someone is outgoing, they are friendly and energetic and find it easy and enjoyable to be with others (units 1, 4)
- outspoken — adj if someone is outspoken, they express their opinions even though other people might be offended by them (units 4, 40)
- outstretched — adj if someone’s arms are outstretched they are held out in front of the person’s body, often in order to greet someone (units 4)
- overpriced — adj too expensive (units 4)
- tired out — adj completely exhausted (units 4)
- watered-down — adj when something is made less strong in order to make people agree with it (units 4)
- wear out (sth) or wear (sth) out — to use something so much that it becomes weak or damaged and cannot be used any more, or to become weak and damaged in this way (units 4)
- worked up — adj if you are worked-up, you feel very upset, nervous or excited about something (units 4)
- worn-out — adj weak and damaged through much use (units 1, 4)
