### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- cast-offs — n clothes which have been given to somebody else because the first owner cannot use them any more (units 38)
- dress up (sb) or dress (sb) up — to put on someone else’s clothes to make yourself look like someone else, or to make someone do this, usually as a game (units 38)
- fling off sth or fling sth off — to remove very quickly (units 38)
- get into sth — informal to be thin enough to be able to put your clothes on (units 38)
- getup — n informal the particular clothing, especially when strange or unusual, that someone is wearing (units 38)
- have on sth or have sth on — if you have clothes or shoes on, you are wearing them (units 38)
- let out sth or let sth out — to make a piece of clothing wider by removing the sewing from the side edges and then sewing closer to the edge of the material (units 38)
- let down sth or let sth down — to make a piece of clothing longer by removing the sewing from the bottom folded edge and then sewing closer to the edge of the material (units 38)
- pull on sth — to put on clothes quickly (units 38)
- roll up sth or roll sth up — to fold the edges of a piece of clothing that you are wearing [e.g. sleeves, trousers] in order to make them shorter (units 38)
- smooth down sth or smooth sth down — to press your hair or your clothes with your hands in order to make them flat (units 38)
- take up sth or take sth up — to shorten a piece of clothing [e.g. skirt, trousers] (units 38)
- tie back sth or tie sth back — to fasten something that usually hangs down [esp. hair] so that it is fixed in position and not hanging down (units 2, 38)
- zip up sth or zip sth up — to fasten a piece of clothing by using its zip (= a long metal or plastic fastener), or to help someone close the zip on a piece of clothing they are wearing (units 38)
