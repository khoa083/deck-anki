### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- buoy up sth or buoy sth up — to support something and help to make it more successful (units 22)
- cave in — to agree to something that you were against before, after someone has persuaded you or threatened you (units 22, 23)
- cheer on sb or cheer sb on — to shout encouraging words at someone, especially a person or team in a race or competition, or to receive encouraging words or shouts (units 22)
- come out against — to state publicly that you are opposed to an issue (units 22)
- come out in favour of — to state publicly that you support an issue (units 22)
- cross over — to start to support a different, often opposing, person or group (units 22)
- defer to sb/sth — formal to accept someone else’s opinion because they know more than you or are more important than you (units 22, 23)
- egg on sb or egg sb on — to encourage someone to do something, often something that is wrong, stupid, or dangerous, or to be encouraged to do something (units 22)
- go with sth — to accept a plan or an idea, or to support the person whose ideas you agree with (units 22, 23)
- not hear of sth or not hear of sb doing sth — to not allow something, or not allow someone to do something (units 22)
- lean towards sth/doing sth — to support, or begin to support, a particular set of ideas or a particular political party (units 22)
- side against sb — to oppose a person or group in an argument (units 22)
- side with sb — to support a person or group in an argument (units 22)
- stand for sth — if a group of people stand for a set of ideas, they support those ideas, or if something stands for a particular idea, it represents that idea (units 22)
- stick by sth — to continue to support or use a decision, opinion, or plan (units 22)
- stick up for sth — to defend or fight for something important [e.g. rights] (units 22)
- swear by sth — to believe that something is very effective and that it will always work well (units 22)
