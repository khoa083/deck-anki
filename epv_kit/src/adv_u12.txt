English Phrasal Verbs in Use Advanced

### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- back off — to stop being involved in a situation, especially in order to allow other people to deal with it themselves (units 12)
- cordon off sth or cordon sth off — if people in authority [e.g. police] cordon off an area, they put something around it in order to stop people from entering it (units 12)
- cut off sth or cut sth off — to stop the supply of something such as electricity, gas or water (units 2, 12)
- cut off (from) swh — to be in a separate space or area and unable to communicate with people in a different space or area (units 12)
- finish off sb or finish sb off — to make someone so tired, weak, or unhappy that they are unable to continue what they were doing (units 12)
- have off sth or have sth off — to spend time away from work (units 12)
- kick off (sth) — informal to start (units 12)
- kick off sth or kick sth off — to remove your shoes by shaking your feet (units 12)
- leave off (sth/doing sth) — to stop, or to stop doing something (units 12)
- round off sth or round sth off — to do something as a way of finishing an event or activity in a satisfactory way (units 12, 33)
- shake off sb or shake sb off — to succeed in escaping from someone who is following you (units 12)
- Shove off! — something that you say when you are angry to tell someone to go away (units 12)
- show off — to show someone or something that you are proud of to a group of people (units 12)
- shrug off sth or shrug sth off — to not worry about something and treat it as unimportant (units 12)
- split off — to stop belonging to a particular group or political party and form a separate one (units 12)
- start sb off — to help someone to start an activity, especially a piece of work (units 12)
- switch off — to stop giving your attention to something or someone (units 12)
- tick off sb or tick sb off — British & Australian to tell someone that they have done something wrong and that you are angry about it (units 12)
- work off sth or work sth off — to do something energetic to stop yourself becoming fat after eating a lot of food (units 12)
