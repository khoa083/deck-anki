### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- bring together sb or bring sb together — to cause people to be friendly with each other, especially people who have argued with each other or who would not usually be friendly with each other (units 39)
- bust-up — n informal a break in a relationship (units 39)
- butter up sb or butter sb up — informal to be very nice to someone so that they will do what you want them to do (units 39)
- cheat on sb — informal to behave in a dishonest way towards your husband, wife, or usual sexual partner by having a sexual relationship with someone else (units 39)
- come between sb — if an argument or other problem comes between two or more people, it spoils their relationship (units 39)
- come out — to go somewhere with someone for a social event (units 39)
- flirt with sb — to talk and behave towards someone in a way that is sexually attractive and which shows that person that you are sexually attracted to them (units 39)
- fuss over sb/sth — to pay too much attention to someone or something, especially because you want to show that you like them (units 39)
- go out together — to have a romantic relationship (units 39)
- go out with sb — to have a romantic relationship (units 39)
- hear from sb — to receive news or information from someone, usually by letter or telephone (units 39)
- have/had heard of sb/sth — to know a little about someone or something because you have read, seen, or been told something about them before (units 39)
- know of sb/sth — to have heard of someone or something and to be able to give some information about them, but not very much (units 39)
- be mixed up with sb — to be involved with someone who has a bad influence on you (units 39)
- patch up sth or patch sth up — to try to improve your relationship with someone after an argument (units 39)
- strike up sth — to start a conversation or relationship with someone (units 39)
- suck up to sb — informal to try to make someone who is in a position of authority like you by doing and saying things that will please them (units 39)
- warm to sb — to start to like someone (units 39)
