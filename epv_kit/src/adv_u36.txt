### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- bail out sb/sth or bail sb/sth out — to help a person or organisation by giving them money (units 6, 36)
- break into sth — to start to use an amount of money or food that you have been saving (units 36)
- carry forward sth or carry sth forward — to include an amount of money in a later set of calculations (units 6, 36)
- cash up — British & Australian to count all the money taken by a shop or business at the end of the day (units 36)
- chip in (sth) or chip (sth) in — slightly informal to give an amount of money, especially when a group of people are giving money to pay for something together (units 36)
- clean out sb or clean sb out — informal if someone cleans you out, they take or use all the money you have, or if something expensive cleans you out, you spend all the money you have on it (units 36)
- come into sth — to get money from someone who has died (units 36)
- cough up (sth) or cough (sth) up — informal to provide money for something, especially when you are not very willing to do this (units 36)
- gobble up sth or gobble sth up — informal to use a lot of something, especially money (units 36)
- pay sth into sth — to put money into a bank account, often to save money for a particular purpose (units 36)
- plough back sth or plough sth back — to put money that you have earned into a business, in order to make the business bigger or better (units 36)
- put aside sth/sb or push sth/sb — to save money for a particular purpose (units 36)
- put by sth or put sth by — to save an amount of money in order to use it later (units 36)
- rent out sth or rent sth out — if you rent out something that you own [e.g. house], you allow someone to pay you money so that they can use it (units 36)
- roll in — if money or requests for money [e.g. bills] roll in, they arrive in large numbers (units 36)
- run through sth — if you run through money, you spend a lot of it very quickly (units 36)
- scrape by — to manage to live when you do not have much money (units 36)
- set sb back (sth) — informal to cost someone a particular amount of money, usually a large amount of money (units 36)
- square up — informal to pay someone the money you owe them (units 6, 36)
- stake sth on sth/doing sth — to risk losing money or harming something important [e.g. reputation] if a plan does not succeed (units 36)
- work off sth or work sth off — to reduce the size of a debt, either by earning money to pay for it or by working for the person you owe money to (units 36)
