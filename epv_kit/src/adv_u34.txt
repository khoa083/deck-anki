### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- aim at sth/doing sth — to intend to achieve something, or to be intended to achieve something (units 34)
- base sth on sth — if you base something on particular facts or ideas, you use those facts or ideas to develop that thing (units 6, 34)
- cater for sb/sth — to provide all the things that people need or want in a particular situation (units 34)
- consist of sth — to be formed from two or more things (units 34)
- contend with sth — to have to deal with a difficult or unpleasant situation (units 2, 34)
- deprive sb/sth of sth — to take something important away from someone (units 2, 34)
- detract from sth — to make something seem less good than it really is or than it was thought to be (units 34)
- devote sth to sth/doing sth — to use all of something, for example your life or your time, for a particular purpose (units 34)
- be founded on sth — to be based on a particular idea or belief (units 34)
- improve on sth — to do something in a better way or with better results than when it was done before (units 34)
- insist on sth/doing sth — to demand something and to make it clear that you will not accept anything else (units 34)
- interest sb in sth — to try to persuade someone that they want something (units 34)
- invest sth in sth — to use a lot of time or effort trying to achieve something or trying to make something successful (units 34)
- object to sb/sth/doing sth — to feel or say that you oppose or dislike something or someone (units 34)
- profit from sth/doing sth — to get an advantage from something (units 34)
- refer to sb/sth — if writing or information refers to someone or something, it describes or is about that person or thing (units 34)
- relate to sth — to be connected to a particular subject, or to be about a particular subject (units 34)
- result in sth — to cause something to happen, or to make a situation exist (units 17, 34)
- sum up sb/sth or sum sth/sb up — if something sums up someone or something, it represents the most typical qualities of that person or thing (units 34)
