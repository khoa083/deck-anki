### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- arrive at sth — to achieve an agreement or decision, especially after thinking about it or discussing it for a long time (units 29)
- come around/round — to agree to a plan or idea that you were against, or to stop having a bad opinion about something, after thinking about it for a long time or being persuaded by other people that it is good (units 29)
- come round to — to agree with somebody else’s idea or opinion after a long time thinking about it or discussing it (units 23, 29)
- decide on sth/sb — to choose something or someone after thinking carefully (units 29)
- do in sb or do sb in — informal to attack or kill someone (units 29)
- do sb out of sth — informal to stop someone from getting or keeping something, in a dishonest or unfair way (units 29)
- do yourself up to make yourself look more attractive — 29 do without (sth/sb) to manage without something or someone (units 29)
- drum up sth — to increase interest in something or support for something (units 29)
- mull over sth or mull sth over — to think carefully about something for a long time, often before making a decision (units 29)
- play sb off against sb — to encourage one person or group to compete against or argue with another, hoping that you can get some advantage from this situation (units 29)
- play on sth — if someone plays on your fears or weaknesses, they use them to try and make you do what they want, often in a way that is unfair (units 29)
- push through sth or push sth through — to make a plan or suggestion be officially accepted (units 29)
- reason with sb — to try to persuade someone not to do something stupid by giving them good reasons not to (units 29)
- single out sb/sth or single sb/sth out — to choose one person or thing from a large group in order to criticise or praise them (units 29)
- size up sb/sth or size sb/sth up — to carefully examine a situation or person in order to make a judgement (units 29)
- tend towards sth — to be likely to choose a particular thing (units 29)
- tie sb down — to stop someone from being free to do what they want to do (units 10, 29)
- toss (sb) for sth — to decide which person or team can do something or have something by throwing a coin in the air and guessing which side of the coin will be on top when it lands (units 29)
- toy with sth — to consider something or doing something, but not in a very serious way and without making a decision (units 29)
- work out sth or work sth out — to think carefully about how you are going to do something and to make a plan or decision (units 1, 29)
