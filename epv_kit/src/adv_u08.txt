Hi Millie,
How’s your awful new boss? Still planning a total reorganisation of the office or have you 
persuaded her not to throw the baby out with the bathwater1? Let me know if you need an 
evening out to let off steam2 – it’s about time we met up again.
We’ve been told at our company that we won’t be getting a salary increase this year. They took 
the sting out of it3 by giving us a Christmas bonus, but people are not happy.
My boss is still as difficult as ever. Her personal assistant’s resigned, but I think that might be 
cutting off her nose to spite her face4 as she’ll find it hard to find anything else as well-paid. 
She can usually run rings round5 anyone, so it must have been a shock for her to get a boss she 
couldn’t manipulate. 
I badly need your advice. Joey’s beginning to turn up the heat in our relationship – he’s dropping 
hints about marriage. He invited me to meet his parents last weekend. We got on like a house on 
fire6 but I just don’t feel ready to put down roots yet. Should I stop seeing him? I don’t want to but 
maybe it’d be kinder? I can’t make up my mind. Let me know what you think.
Lydia

### MINI DICTIONARY (định nghĩa chính thức của sách cho các mục thuộc unit này):
- clean up your act — to start to behave better (units 8)
- come into one’s own — to be very successful (units 8)
- cut off your nose to spite your face — to do something because you are angry, though it may cause you more problems (units 8)
- dig your heels in — to refuse to do what others try to persuade you to do (units 8)
- get off on the wrong foot — to start something badly (units 8)
- get on like a house on fire — to immediately like each other (units 8)
- get your act together — to organise yourself more efficiently (units 8, 57)
- be going round in circles — to use a lot of time and effort with no results (units 8)
- let off steam — to talk or act in a way that helps get rid of strong feelings (units 8)
- make up for lost time — to do something to compensate for not doing it previously (units 8)
- make up your mind — to make a decision about something (units 8)
- pull out all the stops — to do everything you can (units 8)
- put down roots — to settle down and make a relationship more permanent (units 8)
- put in a good word for someone — to say good things about someone to people in authority (units 8)
- run rings round — to outwit and be cleverer than people (units 8)
- take the sting out of something — to make something that is unpleasant less so (units 8)
- throw the baby out with the bathwater — to get rid of the good parts of something as well as the bad parts (units 8)
- turn up the heat — to make a relationship more intense (units 8)
- wake up to the fact — to be realistic (units 8)
