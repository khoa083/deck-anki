### MINI DICTIONARY (OCR lại 300 dpi, mỗi dòng = 1 mục; số trong ngoặc = các unit):
- ase, bring sb around/round to persuade someone to agree with you or to do what you want them todo (39)
- brush aside sb/sth or brush sb/ sth aside to refuse to listen to what someone says, or to refuse to think about something seriously (2, 39)
- call for sth to say that you think a particular thing should be done, usually in order to change or improve a situation (39)
- fall for sth to be tricked into believing something that is not true (2, 39)
- have sb on to persuade someone that something is true when it is not, usually as ajoke (39)
- press (sb) for sth to try to persuade someone, usually someone in authority, to give you something or to allow something to happen (39)
- put across sth or put sth across to explain or express something clearly so that people understand iteasily (11, 39)
- put forward sth or put sth forward to state an idea or opinion, or to suggest a plan, So that it can be considered or discussed (39)
- put over sth or put sth over to express an idea clearly so that people understand it (17, 39)
- put sth to sb to suggest an idea or plan to someone so that they can consider it or discuss it (39)
- rope in sb or rope sb in informal to persuade someone to help you with something, especially when they do not want to (39)
- talk sb around/round to persuade someone to agree with you or to do what you want them to do (39)
- talk sb into sth/doing sth to persuade someone to do something (39)
- talk sb out of sth/doing sth to persuade someone not to do something (39)
