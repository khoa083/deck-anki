2 Inthe sentences where the word up is not essential, using it seems to add a meaning of:
- either something being done completely (tidy up, clear up, jumble up, load up, clog up, eat up,
drink up, use up).
- or something being made into lots of small pieces (divide up, chop up). This meaning can be
seen in verbs such as smash up, cut up, tear up.
